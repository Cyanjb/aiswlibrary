Chartreuse

A sharp yellow-green, halfway between lime and lemon, named after a French herbal liqueur.

Duochrome

A finish that shifts between two colors as you move, like a beetle shell or shimmery eyeshadow going from purple to green.

Prussian Blue

A deep, inky blue with a touch of green, made in Berlin around 1706.

Tyrian Purple

An ancient reddish purple dye made from sea snails.

Ultramarine

A deep, glowing blue first made by grinding the stone lapis lazuli.

Vantablack

One of the darkest materials ever made, a black so deep it swallows almost all light.

Verdigris

The chalky blue-green crust that grows on old copper, brass and bronze, like on old statues and church roofs.

YInMn Blue

A vivid, electric blue pigment discovered by accident in a university lab in 2009.
