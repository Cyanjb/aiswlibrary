Antoni Gaudi

Spanish architect (1852-1926) whose buildings look like they melted, sprouted or grew from the sea.

Frank Lloyd Wright

American architect (1867-1959) whose houses hug the land with long, low roofs, warm wood, stone and bands of windows.

Le Corbusier

Swiss-French architect (1887-1965) who made clean white boxes lifted on slim pillars, with long strip windows and flat roof gardens.

Louis Kahn

American architect (1901-1974) whose buildings feel like ancient monuments: massive concrete and brick walls, perfect circles and squares, and light pouring through huge cut-out openings.

Ludwig Mies van der Rohe

German-American architect (1886-1969) famous for the motto 'less is more.

Luis Barragan

Mexican architect (1902-1988) known for smooth walls painted hot pink, sunny yellow and deep blue, with still pools of water and sharp shadows.

Oscar Niemeyer

Brazilian architect (1907-2012) who bent white concrete into sweeping curves, domes and bowls.

Zaha Hadid

Iraqi-British architect (1950-2016) known for buildings that swoop and flow like liquid, with no straight corners.
