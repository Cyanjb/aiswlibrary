Architectural Styles

Art Nouveau Architecture

Buildings from around 1900 covered in flowing, plant-like curves: whiplash ironwork, flower-shaped windows and doorways that look like vines.

Bauhaus

Early 1900s German modern style built from simple boxes, flat roofs, big windows and no decoration.

Craftsman Bungalow

A cozy early 1900s American house with a low roof, a deep front porch on thick tapered columns, and lots of natural wood and stone.

Googie

Space age roadside style from 1950s America: boomerang roofs, starbursts, tilted glass and big neon signs on diners, motels and car washes.

Mediterranean Villa

Sun-washed white or warm plaster walls, red clay roof tiles, arched windows, terraces and bright flowers spilling over stone steps.

Mid-Century Modern

The look of 1950s and 60s homes: low flat roofs, walls of glass, warm wood and open rooms that flow out to the garden.

Moorish Architecture

Palaces and courtyards from Islamic Spain and North Africa, with horseshoe arches, carved plaster lace, patterned tiles and quiet fountains.

Tudor Revival

Storybook houses with dark wooden beams crossing white walls, steep pointed roofs, tall chimneys and small diamond-paned windows.
