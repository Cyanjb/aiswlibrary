Art Handicrafts

8 keywords in this category, each one tested and explained in plain language.

Beadwork

Designs made from tiny glass beads sewn or strung together, sparkling in bright rows and patterns.

Embroidery

Pictures and patterns stitched with colored thread onto fabric, with raised, glossy stitches you can almost feel.

Macrame

Thick cotton cord knotted by hand into wall hangings, plant hangers and fringes with lots of soft texture.

Marquetry

Pictures made by fitting thin slices of different colored woods together like a jigsaw on furniture or panels.

Mosaic

Pictures made from hundreds of tiny tiles, glass or broken pottery pieces set side by side, with thin lines of grout between them.

Needlepoint

Tiny even stitches of wool worked across a stiff canvas grid, filling every space to make a soft, pixel-like picture.

Quilting

Blankets sewn from many fabric patches into patterns like stars, squares and zigzags, with soft puffy stitching.

Weaving

Threads crossed over and under on a loom to make cloth, rugs and wall hangings, with visible texture and stripes.
