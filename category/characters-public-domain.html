Count Dracula

The vampire from the original 1897 novel: a tall old man dressed all in black, with a long white moustache, pointed ears, sharp teeth and hair on his palms.

Dorothy of Oz

Frankenstein's Creature

The being from the 1818 novel: very tall, with yellowish skin, flowing black hair, watery eyes and dark lips, gentle and well-spoken at first.

Pinocchio

The wooden puppet from the original 1883 Italian story, carved by an old woodcarver, with a nose that grows when he lies.

Sherlock Holmes
