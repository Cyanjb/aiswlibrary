Emotions and Moods

8 keywords in this category, each one tested and explained in plain language.

Awe

The feeling of standing before something enormous and beautiful: a tiny figure under a sky full of stars, a waterfall, a cathedral, a whale.

Defiance

Standing firm against something bigger: chin up, fists clenched, wind in the hair, looking straight back at the storm or the crowd.

Euphoria

Pure, bursting joy: arms thrown wide, heads tipped back laughing, color and light everywhere.

Longing

Wanting something just out of reach: a face pressed to a window, a hand on a closed door, someone watching a ship sail away.

Melancholy

A soft, thoughtful sadness, like rain on a window or the last day of summer.

Nostalgia

That warm ache for the past: faded photos, sunlit bedrooms, old toys and summer afternoons that seem to last forever.

Tenderness

Soft, caring closeness: a parent tucking in a child, hands cupping a baby bird, two foreheads touching.

Unease

That quiet feeling that something isn't right.
