Crystal Caverns

Huge caves filled with giant glowing crystals in every color, sparkling like the inside of a geode.

Dieselpunk

A gritty, alternate past powered by oil and engines, inspired by the 1920s to 1940s.

Lunarpunk

The nighttime sister of solarpunk, a peaceful, nature-loving future lit by moonlight, glowing plants and soft lanterns.

Overgrown Ruins

Old buildings swallowed by nature, with vines, trees and moss growing through broken walls and windows.

Sky Islands

Floating chunks of land drifting in the clouds, with waterfalls spilling off their edges into the sky.

Solarpunk

A bright, hopeful future where cities are covered in gardens, solar panels and clean energy.

Steampunk

A Victorian-style world run on steam, with brass gears, goggles, clockwork gadgets and airships.

Underwater City

A whole town under the sea, with glass domes, glowing windows and fish drifting past the streets.
