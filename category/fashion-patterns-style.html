Fashion Patterns and Style

8 keywords in this category, each one tested and explained in plain language.

Pick keywords as you browse, then press Copy for AI and paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI. Copy keywords gives a plain list for Midjourney or Seedance.

Athleisure

Sporty clothes worn as everyday wear: leggings, joggers, zip-up tops, soft hoodies and clean sneakers.

Boho Chic

Free-spirited, layered style: flowing maxi skirts, fringed suede, embroidered tops, wide-brim hats and stacks of jewelry.

Cottage Dress

A soft, romantic dress with puffed sleeves, a fitted bodice and tiny floral print, often in cotton or linen.

Monochrome Outfit

An outfit in a single color from head to toe, like all camel, all lilac or all black, mixing different shades and textures of that one color.

Oversized Streetwear

Baggy hoodies, roomy cargo pants, big puffer jackets and chunky sneakers, worn loose and layered.

Power Suit

A bold suit made to command a room: strong padded shoulders, sharp lapels and a confident color like red, white or black.

Quiet Luxury

Simple, expensive-looking clothes with no logos: cashmere knits, camel coats, soft trousers and perfect fit in cream, beige and grey.

Tailored Suit

A jacket and trousers cut to fit the body just right, with crisp shoulders, clean lines and no extra fabric.
