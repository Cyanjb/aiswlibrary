Charcoal and Gold Leaf

Smudgy black charcoal drawing with shining patches of real-looking gold leaf.

Collage and Paint

Torn paper, old photos and magazine bits glued down, then painted over with bold brushstrokes.

Doodles on Photo

A real photo with playful white or colored doodles drawn over it, like hearts, wings, sparkles and little faces.

Embroidered Photo

A printed photo with real-looking stitches sewn right into it, adding thread flowers, halos or colored patterns.

Ink and Watercolor

Crisp pen lines with soft watercolor washes under them.

Paper and Neon

Soft cut-paper shapes lit up by glowing neon tubes, so the cozy and the electric sit side by side.

Pressed Flowers and Ink

Real flattened flowers and leaves combined with ink drawing, so petals become a dress, hair or wings on a sketched figure.

Watercolor Over Photograph

A real photo with loose watercolor washes painted on top, so parts of the picture melt into soft, colorful paint.
