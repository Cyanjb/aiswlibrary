Figure-Ground Illusion

A picture you can read two ways, like a white vase that is also two dark faces looking at each other.

Forced Perspective

A trick where something near the camera lines up with something far away, so a person seems to pinch the moon or hold up a tower.

Hidden Image

A picture with a second picture tucked inside it, like a forest whose trees form a face when you step back.

Impossible Staircase

Stairs that seem to climb forever in a loop, always going up yet ending where they started.

Impossible Triangle

A solid-looking triangle whose three beams join in a way that could never exist in real space.

Infinity Mirror

Two mirrors facing each other, making lights or reflections repeat in a tunnel that seems to go on forever.

Shadow Art

A pile of random objects that casts a shadow of something totally different, like a heap of junk making the shadow of a dancer.

Trompe l'Oeil

French for 'fools the eye'.
