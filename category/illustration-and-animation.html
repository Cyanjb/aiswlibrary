Cutout Animation

Flat Vector Illustration

Today's clean digital illustration: solid color shapes with no outlines, a soft grain texture, friendly characters in expressive poses and plenty of breathing room.

Isometric Illustration

A tidy, angled view where everything is seen from above at the same slant, with nothing shrinking into the distance.

Rotoscope

Animation traced over real filmed movement, so drawn characters move with lifelike weight and grace.

The bouncy cartoon style of the 1920s and 30s, where arms and legs bend like noodles with no elbows or knees.
