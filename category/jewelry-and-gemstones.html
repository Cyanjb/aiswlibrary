Art Nouveau Jewelry

Jewelry from around 1890 to 1910 full of flowing curves: dragonflies, orchids, peacock feathers and women with long hair, often in glowing enamel, opal and gold.

Cabochon

A gemstone polished into a smooth, rounded dome with no sharp facets.

Emerald Cut

A rectangle-shaped gem cut with clipped corners and long, step-like facets.

Filigree

Jewelry made from tiny twisted threads of gold or silver, curled into delicate lace-like patterns full of little open spaces.

Opal

A milky or dark gemstone that flashes little patches of rainbow color as it moves, like fire trapped inside.

Pearls

Smooth, round gems grown inside oysters and mussels, with a soft, glowing shine.

Rose Gold

Gold mixed with copper to give a warm, pinkish blush color.

Turquoise

A sky-blue to green stone, often with dark web-like veins running through it.
