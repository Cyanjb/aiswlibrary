Bioluminescence

Natural glow from living things, like glowing mushrooms, plankton, or deep-sea creatures.

Candlelight

Warm, flickering, intimate light from a small flame.

Chiaroscuro

An old painting term for dramatic light against deep shadow.

Golden Hour

The warm, honey-colored light you get just after sunrise or before sunset.

Neon Glow

Electric, colorful light like city signs at night.

Overcast Light

The soft, even light of a cloudy day.

Rim Light

A thin outline of light around your subject's edges, like they're glowing from behind.

Volumetric Light

Light you can see traveling through the air, like sunbeams through dust or fog.
