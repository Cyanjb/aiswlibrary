The gentle detective story: a small village, a bookshop or bakery, a cat on the counter and a clue hidden among the teacups.

The hand-drawn world map at the front of a fantasy novel: mountains drawn as little peaks, winding rivers, sea monsters in the corners and old-style lettering.

Fairy Tale

The old storybook world of enchanted woods, tiny cottages, talking animals and castles on hills.

Gothic Novel

The mood of an old Gothic book: crumbling castles, candlelit corridors, storms at the window and a heroine holding a lantern in the dark.

Magical Realism

Everyday life with one quiet impossible thing in it, treated as normal.

Penny Dreadful

The cheap, thrilling story papers of Victorian England, full of villains, ghosts and highwaymen.

Southern Gothic

Stories set in the American South, where the beauty is a little rotten.

Sword and Sorcery

Rough, muscle-and-magic adventure stories: lone warriors, ancient ruins, wicked sorcerers and treasure guarded by something terrible.
