Argyle

Rows of diamonds with thin diagonal lines crossing over them.

Chevron

Bold zigzag stripes stacked into rows of V shapes.

Gingham

An even checkered pattern made by crossing stripes of one color with white.

Herringbone

Rows of short slanted lines that flip direction, like the bones of a fish.

Houndstooth

A broken check made of little jagged four-pointed shapes, usually black and white.

Ikat

A woven pattern with soft, feathery edges, because the threads are dyed in sections before weaving.

Infinity Polka Dots

Dots everywhere: bold polka dots covering walls, floors, objects and people until everything melts into one endless pattern, often red and white or yellow and black.

Paisley

Curving teardrop shapes packed with tiny swirls and flowers.

Seigaiha

Overlapping half circles stacked like fish scales, each one a little wave.
