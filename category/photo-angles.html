Frame Within a Frame

Using a doorway, arch, window or branches to make a natural frame around the subject inside the picture.

From Behind

The subject seen from the back, looking out at what they see.

High Selfie Angle

The classic selfie view, with the camera held above the head and tilted down.

Reflection Shot

The scene shown in a mirror, puddle, lake or shop window, sometimes upside down or doubled.

Three-Quarter View

The face or body turned halfway between front and side, so you see both eyes and the curve of the cheek.

Through the Leaves

The photo is taken from behind blurry leaves or flowers close to the lens, so the subject peeks out from soft green shapes.

Through the Window

Looking at the subject through glass, with reflections, raindrops or a window frame between you.

Worm's-Eye View

The view from the very ground looking straight up, as if you were a worm.
