Daguerreotype

The very first popular kind of photograph, from 1839, made on a polished silver mirror.

Disposable Camera

The look of a cheap throwaway film camera: bright on-camera flash, red eyes, slight blur and grainy colors.

Infrared Film

Film that sees invisible light, turning green leaves and grass bright white or candy pink while skies go dark.

Kodachrome

A famous old color slide film with rich reds, deep blues and warm, glowing skin.

Lomography

Photos from cheap toy cameras with heavy dark corners, oversaturated colors and happy little mistakes.

Pinhole Camera

A tiny hole instead of a lens, giving soft, dreamy pictures with dark edges and everything gently in focus.

Polaroid

An instant photo with a thick white border and soft, slightly faded colors.

Tintype

An old portrait printed straight onto a thin metal plate, popular in the 1860s.
