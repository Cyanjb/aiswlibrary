Ansel Adams

(1902-1984) American landscape photographer famous for huge black and white views of mountains, lakes and Yosemite.

Dorothea Lange

(1895-1965) American documentary photographer known for honest, tender portraits of working families during the Great Depression.

Gordon Parks

(1912-2006) American photographer, filmmaker and writer who captured Black American life and the civil rights era for Life magazine.

Henri Cartier-Bresson

(1908-2004) French photographer who mastered the decisive moment, the split second when everything in a street scene lines up just right.

Irving Penn

(1917-2009) American photographer known for elegant fashion portraits and still lifes against plain gray backdrops.

Richard Avedon

(1923-2004) American fashion and portrait photographer known for people posed against plain white backgrounds.

Saul Leiter

(1923-2013) American photographer who shot New York in soft, painterly color.

Vivian Maier

(1926-2009) American street photographer who secretly took well over 100,000 photos while working as a nanny.
