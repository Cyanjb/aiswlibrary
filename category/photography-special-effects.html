Photography Special Effects

8 keywords in this category, each one tested and explained in plain language.

Pick keywords as you browse, then press Copy for AI and paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI. Copy keywords gives a plain list for Midjourney or Seedance.

Freelensing

Taking the lens off the camera and holding it loosely in front.

Intentional Camera Movement

Moving the camera on purpose while the photo is taken, so trees, lights or the sea smear into painterly streaks.

Lensball

A clear glass ball held up in front of a scene, showing a tiny upside-down copy of the world inside while the background blurs.

Light Painting

Drawing with a moving light in the dark while the camera shutter stays open.

Multiple Exposure

Several pictures layered into one frame, so faces, cities and nature blend together like a memory.

Prism Effect

Holding a glass prism in front of the lens to throw rainbows, ghostly double edges and soft color streaks across the picture.

Smoke Bomb

A burst of thick colored smoke filling the air around a subject.

Zoom Burst

Zooming the lens while the photo is taken, so streaks shoot out from the center like you are flying forward.
