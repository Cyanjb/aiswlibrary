Eucalyptus

Round, coin-shaped or long leaves in a soft silvery blue-green, often tied in loose bunches.

Ferns

Soft, feathery fronds that curl open from little spirals, filling shady forest floors in layers of green.

Monstera

A big, glossy houseplant with heart-shaped leaves full of splits and holes.

Rosemary

A woody herb with needle-like, silvery green leaves and tiny pale blue flowers.

Succulents

Plump, fleshy little plants growing in neat rosettes, in shades of sage, blue-green and dusty pink.

Sunflower

Tall stems carrying big golden faces with dark centers, all turned toward the light.

Wild Poppies

Bright red, paper-thin petals with dark centers, nodding in tall grass.

Wisteria

Long, hanging clusters of purple or white flowers dripping from vines over arches and walls.
