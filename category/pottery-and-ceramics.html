Blue and White Porcelain

Smooth white porcelain painted with cobalt blue dragons, flowers and landscapes.

Celadon

A soft jade-green glaze, glassy and pale, used on Chinese and Korean pottery for over a thousand years.

Delftware

Glossy white Dutch pottery painted in soft cobalt blue: windmills, tulips, ships and little country scenes.

Kintsugi

The Japanese art of mending broken pottery with gold, so the cracks become shining golden rivers across the piece.

Majolica

Glossy pottery with a milky white glaze painted in bright yellows, blues, greens and oranges, often with flowers, fruit and swirls.

Raku

Pottery pulled red hot from the kiln, leaving smoky black cracks, crackled white glaze and flashes of copper and rainbow sheen.

Speckled Stoneware

Sturdy handmade mugs and plates in creamy or oatmeal glaze dotted with tiny brown speckles, like a bird egg.

Wood-Fired Pottery

Pots baked for days in a wood-burning kiln, so flying ash melts into drippy glaze and flame marks paint warm browns, oranges and greens.
