Cyanotype

A sun print in deep Prussian blue, where objects laid on special paper leave crisp white ghostly shapes.

Etching

A print made from lines scratched into a metal plate and bitten with acid, so the picture is built from fine crosshatched lines.

Letterpress

Old-school printing with metal or wood type pressed into thick paper, so letters sit in a slight dent with rich, slightly uneven ink.

Linocut

A print carved into a block of lino, so you get bold chunky shapes, white gouge marks and solid flat ink.

Monotype

A one-off print made by painting on a smooth plate and pressing paper onto it, giving soft, smudgy, painterly marks.

Risograph

A grainy stencil printing look with bright see-through inks like fluoro pink and teal that overlap into new colors.

Screen Print

Flat, bold layers of ink pushed through a mesh screen.

Woodblock Print

Pictures printed from carved wooden blocks, one block per color, with flat areas of color and wood grain sometimes showing through.
