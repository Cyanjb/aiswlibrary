Style Suffixes

8 keywords in this category, each one tested and explained in plain language.

Pick keywords as you browse, then press Copy for AI and paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI. Copy keywords gives a plain list for Midjourney or Seedance.

-academia

From dark academia and light academia, -academia wraps a theme in old libraries, tweed, candlelit study and handwritten notes.

-core

Add -core to almost any word and it becomes a whole mood: cottagecore, goblincore, normcore.

-gaze

Taken from shoegaze, a hazy, dreamy style of rock music.

-goth

Add -goth to a word to give it a dark, romantic twist: pastel goth, whimsigoth, bubble goth.

-kei

Kei is Japanese for 'style' or 'type,' used in street fashion names like mori kei and dolly kei.

-luxe

From 'deluxe,' -luxe adds a polished, expensive feeling to any theme: boho luxe, night luxe, coastal luxe.

-punk

From cyberpunk and steampunk, -punk means 'imagine a whole world powered by this.

-wave

Borrowed from music names like vaporwave and synthwave, -wave suggests a retro, nostalgic mood with a strong color palette.
