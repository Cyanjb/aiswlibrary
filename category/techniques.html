Cross-Hatching

Shading made from layers of crisscrossing pen lines.

Dry Brush

A nearly dry brush dragged across paper or canvas, so the paint skips and leaves scratchy, broken streaks.

Gold Leaf

Paper-thin sheets of real gold pressed onto a surface, so parts of the art shine like metal.

Impasto

Paint laid on so thickly that it stands up off the canvas in ridges and peaks.

Palette Knife

Paint spread with a flat metal knife instead of a brush, leaving smooth, chunky slabs of color with sharp edges.

Sfumato

Soft, smoky blending where edges melt into shadow with no hard lines.

Stippling

Pictures built entirely from tiny dots.

Wet-on-Wet

Wet paint dropped onto paper that is already wet, so colors bloom, bleed and feather into each other.
