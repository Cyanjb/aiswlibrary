90s Sitcom Set

The bright living room of a 90s TV comedy: a big couch facing the camera, a kitchen behind it, flat even lighting and a front door everyone bursts through.

Kaiju Film

The classic Japanese giant monster movie: a towering creature stomping through a miniature city, tiny tanks and planes, smoke, sparks and screaming crowds.

Nature Documentary

The patient look of a wildlife film: long lens, soft background, animals going about their day, and the feeling that someone waited hours for this one shot.

Silent Film

Old black and white movies from before sound: flickering grain, jerky fast motion, big expressive acting and title cards with fancy borders.

Spaghetti Western

The dusty Italian-made westerns of the 1960s: squinting eyes in huge close-up, sun-baked desert towns, ponchos and long tense standoffs.

Telenovela

The glossy Latin American TV drama: grand staircases, gasps, tearful close-ups, big jewelry and secrets revealed in a sunny mansion.

VHS Horror

Scary movie footage that looks like it was played on a worn-out home video tape.

Wuxia

Chinese martial arts adventure where swordsmen and swordswomen glide across rooftops, bamboo tops and lakes.
