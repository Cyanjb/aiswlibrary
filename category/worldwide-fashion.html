Ao Dai

Vietnam's national dress: a long, fitted silk tunic with a high collar and side slits up to the waist, worn over loose flowing trousers.

Dashiki

A loose, colorful pullover tunic from West Africa with a richly embroidered or printed neckline.

Hanbok

Traditional Korean clothing.

Huipil

A loose tunic made from rectangles of handwoven cloth, worn by Indigenous women in southern Mexico and Guatemala.

Kente Cloth

Brightly colored cloth from Ghana, made by the Asante and Ewe peoples by weaving narrow strips on a loom and sewing them together.

Kimono

Japan's traditional robe: a straight, T-shaped garment wrapped around the body and tied with a wide sash called an obi.

Qipao

A fitted Chinese dress with a high mandarin collar, side slits and frog-knot buttons, often in silk with embroidered flowers.

Sari

A long, unstitched length of cloth, usually five to nine yards, draped around the body over a fitted blouse, with the decorated end falling over the shoulder.
