1920s Flapper Dress

What it means

Loose, straight dresses that drop to the hips and stop near the knee, covered in beads, sequins or swinging fringe. Think bobbed hair, long pearls and a feathered headband.

When to use it

Use for jazz age parties, speakeasies and dance scenes. Fringe and beads come alive in video when the dancer moves.
