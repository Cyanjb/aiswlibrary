Fashion Timeline and History

1940s Wartime Fashion

What it means

Practical style shaped by fabric rationing: padded shoulders, knee-length skirts, neat tailored jackets, headscarves and pinned-up victory roll hairstyles.

When to use it

Use for World War II era home front scenes, factories, train stations and letters home. Add the setting so it does not drift into the 1950s.
