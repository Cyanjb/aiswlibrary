Fashion Timeline and History

1950s New Look

What it means

The postwar silhouette made famous by Christian Dior: soft rounded shoulders, a tiny cinched waist and a full skirt that swings below the knee. Polished, feminine and very structured.

When to use it

Use for 1950s glamour, diners, garden parties and vintage ads. Add gloves, a hat or a petticoat for even more period detail.
