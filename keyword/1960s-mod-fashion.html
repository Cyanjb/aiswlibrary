1960s Mod Fashion

What it means

The bold London look of the mid 1960s: short shift dresses, mini skirts, bright color blocks, black and white graphics, go-go boots and sharp geometric haircuts.

When to use it

Use for playful, graphic fashion with a youthful swagger. Works best with clean, simple backgrounds that let the shapes pop.
