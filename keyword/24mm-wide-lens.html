Lenses and Optics

24mm Wide Lens

What it means

A wide lens that fits lots of the scene into the frame. It makes the viewer feel like they are standing right inside the action.

When to use it

Use it to show off a place, or to get close to action while still seeing the whole room. Faces near the edges can look a little stretched, so keep people toward the middle.
