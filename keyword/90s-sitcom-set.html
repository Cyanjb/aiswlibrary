TV and Movie Culture

90s Sitcom Set

What it means

The bright living room of a 90s TV comedy: a big couch facing the camera, a kitchen behind it, flat even lighting and a front door everyone bursts through.

When to use it

Use for funny, warm, nostalgic scenes where people gather and chat. The flat lighting is part of the charm, so do not expect moody shadows.
