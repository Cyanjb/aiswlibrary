Web and App Design Styles

Agentic UI

What it means

A calm, chat-first screen where you tell an AI helper what you want and it does the steps for you. Very few buttons, clear results, and elegant Playfair Display headings with a code-style font for small details.

When to use it

Use for AI assistant apps, booking helpers or any tool where people hand off a task. Less suited to sites where people want to browse and click around freely.
