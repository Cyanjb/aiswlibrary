Albrecht Durer

What it means

(1471-1528) German artist best known for incredibly fine engravings and woodcuts, plus careful nature studies like his famous hare. Crisp lines and patient detail.

When to use it

Use for intricate black ink engravings, old book prints and detailed animal studies. Add the word engraving to get his line work.
