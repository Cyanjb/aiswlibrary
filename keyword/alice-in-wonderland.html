Alice in Wonderland

What it means

The curious girl who falls down the rabbit hole, as drawn in the original book's pen and ink pictures: long hair, puffed sleeves and a pinafore over her dress. Describe the book version, not later cartoon or film versions.

When to use it

Use for dreamy, topsy-turvy scenes with odd creatures and impossible tea parties. Say 'Victorian engraving style' for the classic book feel.
