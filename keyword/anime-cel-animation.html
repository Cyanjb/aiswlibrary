Motion Graphics Styles

Anime Cel Animation

What it means

TV anime look with crisp linework, hard-edged shadows and painted skies. Calm held poses suddenly snap into action with speed lines, a quick black and white impact flash and a little camera shake.

When to use it

Great for action-packed reels, game trailers and dramatic reveals. It needs strong key poses to work, and the impact tricks lose punch if you use them on every beat.

Try this
"10-second action reel, anime cel animation, hero holds then snaps into a punch with radial speed lines and an impact flash"
