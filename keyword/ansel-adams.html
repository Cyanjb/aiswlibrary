Photographers

Ansel Adams

What it means

(1902-1984) American landscape photographer famous for huge black and white views of mountains, lakes and Yosemite. Deep blacks, bright whites and every gray in between.

When to use it

Use for grand, crisp black and white nature scenes. He shot landscapes, so it helps less with people or color.
