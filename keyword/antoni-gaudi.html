Architects

Antoni Gaudi

What it means

Spanish architect (1852-1926) whose buildings look like they melted, sprouted or grew from the sea. Wavy walls, bone-shaped columns and broken tile mosaics in every color.

When to use it

Use when you want a building that feels alive and dreamlike. It can get busy fast, so keep the rest of the prompt simple.

Try this
"cathedral interior like a stone forest, Antoni Gaudi style, colored light"
