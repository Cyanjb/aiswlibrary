Worldwide Fashion

Ao Dai

What it means

Vietnam's national dress: a long, fitted silk tunic with a high collar and side slits up to the waist, worn over loose flowing trousers. Simple, slim and graceful.

When to use it

Use for portraits, city streets and Lunar New Year scenes. Ao dai is worn for Tet, weddings and as a school uniform, and the long panels look lovely moving in the wind.
