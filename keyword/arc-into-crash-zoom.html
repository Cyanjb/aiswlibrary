Camera Movements

Arc Into Crash Zoom

What it means

The camera curves slowly around the subject, then zooms in hard and fast.

When to use it

Use it as punctuation for a big moment. The sudden change in speed is the whole effect, so keep the arc truly slow.

Try this
"a slow arc halfway round a robot, then a sudden crash zoom onto its glowing eye"
