Art Deco and Nouveau Animation

What it means

A 1920s poster engraving itself in gold: symmetrical ornament lines draw outward from the center, sunbursts fan open and elegant type rises into its frame. Everything moves slowly and stately, with no bounce.

When to use it

Lovely for luxury brands, invitations, gala openers and vintage title cards. Its slow grandeur can feel stiff for casual or fast social content.
