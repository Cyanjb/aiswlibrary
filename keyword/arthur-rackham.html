Illustrators

Arthur Rackham

What it means

(1867-1939) English illustrator of fairy tales and Peter Pan, full of twisted trees, gnarled roots and tiny fairies. Fine ink lines and faded, earthy watercolor.

When to use it

Use for spooky but sweet fairy tale scenes and old storybook pages. The colors are muted, so it will not give you bright pops.
