Athleisure

What it means

Sporty clothes worn as everyday wear: leggings, joggers, zip-up tops, soft hoodies and clean sneakers. Comfy, fresh and ready for anything.

When to use it

Use for lifestyle, coffee runs, yoga and wellness scenes. Describe colors and fabrics instead of brand names.
