Body Types

Athletic Build

What it means

A strong, fit body with visible muscle from sport or training. Shoulders, arms and legs look capable and ready to move.

When to use it

Use for sports, fitness and action scenes for any age or gender. If results look like bodybuilders, say "lean athletic build" or name the sport.
