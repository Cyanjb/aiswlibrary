Illustrators

Aubrey Beardsley

What it means

(1872-1898) English illustrator of bold black and white drawings with sweeping curves, big flat black shapes and very fine lines. Elegant, strange and a little wicked.

When to use it

Use for striking ink posters, decadent portraits and gothic book art. It is pure black and white, so color prompts fight it.
