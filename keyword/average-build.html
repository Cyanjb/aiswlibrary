Average Build

What it means

An ordinary, everyday body: not model-thin, not bulky, just the shape most people actually have. Soft in some places, firm in others.

When to use it

Use when the AI keeps giving you glossy, perfect bodies and you want real people. Great for lifestyle and stock-style scenes that need to feel relatable.
