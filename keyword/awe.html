Emotions and Moods

Awe

What it means

The feeling of standing before something enormous and beautiful: a tiny figure under a sky full of stars, a waterfall, a cathedral, a whale.

When to use it

Use when you want the viewer to feel small in a good way. Make the person tiny and the wonder huge for the biggest effect.

Try this
"awe, tiny hiker beneath a vast glowing aurora, snowy peaks"
