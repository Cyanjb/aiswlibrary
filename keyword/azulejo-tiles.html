Azulejo Tiles

What it means

Painted, glazed ceramic tiles that cover walls, churches and whole building fronts in Portugal. Best known in cobalt blue on white, with swirling flowers and painted scenes.

When to use it

Use for sunny architecture, kitchens, patterns and travel scenes. Say 'blue and white' for the classic look, since azulejos come in many colors.
