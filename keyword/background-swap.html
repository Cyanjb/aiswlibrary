Transitions and Editing

Background Swap

What it means

The subject stays exactly in place while the world behind them changes.

When to use it

Use to show time passing or a life changing around one person.

Try this
"he stays still while the background cuts from beach to city to snow"
Pairs well with

jump-cut · match-cut

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
