World Culture Styles

Batik

What it means

Cloth decorated with a wax-resist method: wax blocks the dye in some places, leaving the pattern behind. Famous in Indonesia, especially Java, often with fine crackle lines where the wax broke.

When to use it

Use for flowing textile patterns, fabric backgrounds and clothing. Mention colors and motifs, like flowers, birds or waves, to guide it.

"batik fabric with birds and vines, indigo and brown"
