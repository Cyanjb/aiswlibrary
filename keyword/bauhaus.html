Architectural Styles

Bauhaus

What it means

Early 1900s German modern style built from simple boxes, flat roofs, big windows and no decoration. Often white walls with pops of red, yellow and blue.

When to use it

Use for clean, honest buildings and graphic, geometric scenes. It is very plain on purpose, so add color or people if it feels empty.
