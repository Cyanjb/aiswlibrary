Beatrix Potter

What it means

(1866-1943) English author and illustrator of Peter Rabbit. Small, gentle watercolors of animals in tiny coats, with real-looking fur and quiet country gardens.

When to use it

Use for sweet children's book animals and cozy garden scenes. It works best small and simple, not for big epic scenes.
