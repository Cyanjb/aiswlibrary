Before and After Slider

What it means

Two versions of the same picture sit on top of each other, with a line and handle you drag sideways to uncover one or the other.

When to use it

Use it to show a change, like a photo edit, a makeover or a redesign. It needs two pictures lined up exactly, or the effect falls apart.
