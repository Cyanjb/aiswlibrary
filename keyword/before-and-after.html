Storytelling

Before and After

What it means

One frame split in two, or two moments side by side, showing how something has changed. A bare plot then a full garden, a messy room then a tidy one.

When to use it

Use to show transformation, growth or the passing of time. Say 'split screen' or 'side by side' clearly, or the model may only show one side.

Try this
"before and after, split screen, empty lot and blooming community garden"

match cut · morph · wide shot
