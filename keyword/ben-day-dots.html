Ben-Day Dots

What it means

Tiny evenly spaced colored dots used in old printed comics to make pinks, skies and skin tones. Up close they make a bold, poppy pattern.

When to use it

Use for retro pop art and classic comic looks. Ask for 'large visible dots' if you want them to really show.
