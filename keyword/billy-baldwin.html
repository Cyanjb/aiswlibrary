Billy Baldwin

What it means

American decorator (1903-1983) known for glossy chocolate brown walls, tall brass bookshelves, simple cotton slipcovers and comfy slipper chairs. Classy but never fussy.

When to use it

Use for sophisticated, cozy apartments with a 1960s New York feel. Leans dark and warm, so it suits evening scenes.
