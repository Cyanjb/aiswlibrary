Blueprint

What it means

Crisp white lines on deep blue paper, like an old building plan or machine drawing. Measurements, arrows and grid lines make anything look engineered.

When to use it

Use to make a design, invention or dream house feel planned and real. Works best for objects and buildings, less well for faces.
