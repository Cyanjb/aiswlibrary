Business

Boardroom

What it means

A formal meeting room with a long polished table, rows of chairs, a big screen and often a city view behind glass walls.

When to use it

Use for leadership, big decisions and serious business moments. Add people and a mood, or it can feel cold and empty.

"executives around a long boardroom table at dusk, city lights through glass walls"
