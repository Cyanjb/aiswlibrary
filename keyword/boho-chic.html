Boho Chic

What it means

Free-spirited, layered style: flowing maxi skirts, fringed suede, embroidered tops, wide-brim hats and stacks of jewelry. Earthy, relaxed and a little wild.

When to use it

Use for festivals, desert road trips and sunny outdoor portraits. Pairs well with warm, dusty light and natural textures.
