Boho Interior

What it means

Free-spirited rooms with rattan, woven rugs, macrame, floor cushions, trailing plants and warm earthy colors.

When to use it

Use for relaxed, sunny, traveler-style spaces. Easy to overdo, so pick a few hero pieces.
