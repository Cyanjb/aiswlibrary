Bonsai

What it means

A tree kept small in a shallow pot, trimmed and shaped to look old and windswept. Quiet, patient and full of character.

When to use it

Use for calm, minimal scenes and Japanese-inspired still lifes. It shines on a plain background where its shape can breathe.
