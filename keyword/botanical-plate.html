Botanical Plate

What it means

An old scientific page showing one plant from root to flower, drawn with care and labeled in small neat writing. Usually soft colors on cream paper, with a few cut-open details on the side.

When to use it

Use for elegant prints, wall art and anything that should feel studied and calm. Labels may come out as pretend Latin, which often looks charming anyway.
