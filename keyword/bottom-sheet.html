UI Components

Bottom Sheet

What it means

A panel that slides up from the bottom of a phone screen with a small grab handle on top. You can drag it down to close it.

When to use it

Use it on mobile for quick choices like sharing or sorting, right where a thumb can reach. It is made for phones and feels odd on a wide desktop screen.

Try this
"Add a bottom sheet to my mobile app that slides up when I tap Share, shows three options and closes when I drag its handle down"
