Box Braids

What it means

Long braids sectioned into neat squares at the scalp, a protective style with deep roots in African and Black hair traditions. Worn down, up, or gathered into a high bun.

When to use it

Use for accurate, respectful portraits and characters with braided hair. Add length or details, like 'waist-length' or 'with gold cuffs.'
