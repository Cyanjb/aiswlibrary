Breakdancing Freeze

What it means

A breakdancer stopped mid-move, balanced on one hand or their head with legs locked in the air. Bold, strong and surprising.

When to use it

Use for street culture, high-energy scenes and urban portraits. Balances can look awkward, so a low angle helps sell the power.
