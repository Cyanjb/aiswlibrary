Body Types

Broad-Shouldered

What it means

Wide shoulders and a sturdy upper body, the kind of frame that fills a doorway or a jacket. Reads strong, solid and dependable.

When to use it

Use for builders, rowers, swimmers or anyone with a strong frame, of any gender. Helps break the AI habit of giving everyone the same narrow shape.
