Jewelry and Gemstones

Cabochon

What it means

A gemstone polished into a smooth, rounded dome with no sharp facets. It glows from within instead of sparkling.

When to use it

Use for vintage, medieval and fantasy jewelry, especially with moonstone, turquoise or garnet. Great for rings that look old and treasured.
