Web and App Design Styles

Cafe Web Design

What it means

A cozy coffee shop feel with warm browns and creams, soft rounded Poppins type and clean, relaxed layouts.

When to use it

Use for cafes, bakeries, tea shops and small lifestyle brands. A bit too gentle for tech or high-energy products.

Try this
"menu website for a neighborhood bakery, cafe web design, warm cream and coffee brown"
