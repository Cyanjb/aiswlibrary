Storytelling

Call to Adventure

What it means

The moment just before a journey begins: a letter arrives, a door creaks open, a strange light appears on the hill. The character hasn't moved yet, but everything is about to change.

When to use it

Use when you want a picture full of possibility and a story waiting to happen. Show the character small and the doorway or path big.

Try this
"call to adventure, girl at farmhouse gate, glowing path into the woods"
