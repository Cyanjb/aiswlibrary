Calm Before the Storm

What it means

A peaceful scene with a hint of trouble on the way: a picnic under darkening clouds, a sleeping town as an army gathers on the hill.

When to use it

Use to build tension while things still look quiet. Put the calm thing up close and the coming danger small in the distance.

Try this
"calm before the storm, kids flying kites, huge black thunderhead behind"
