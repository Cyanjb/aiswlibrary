Camera Roll World Flip

What it means

The camera rolls a full turn around its lens, and when the horizon comes back level, we are somewhere new.

When to use it

Use it for surreal reveals or a world turning over. Spinning can make some viewers dizzy, so use it once, not in every scene.

Try this
"the camera pushes in and rolls a full turn, the horizon going upside down; as it levels out we are in an underwater city"
