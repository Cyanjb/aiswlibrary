Camera Movements

Car Reveal One-Take

What it means

One unbroken shot that starts from above, drops to the wheel, glides to the lamp, circles to the back and rises to a big wide view.

When to use it

Use it for a whole car or product spot with no cuts. The tested version runs about 20 seconds; for shorter clips, cut it down to two or three steps.

Try this
"one unbroken reveal: top-down over a silver car, descend to the front wheel, glide to the headlamp, orbit to the back, rise to a hero wide"
