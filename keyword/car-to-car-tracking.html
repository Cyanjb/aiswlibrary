Camera Movements

Car-to-Car Tracking

What it means

The camera rides alongside a moving car at the same speed, as if filmed from another vehicle beside it.

When to use it

Use for road trips, car ads and chases. Say the speed and which side the camera is on, or the car may drift out of frame.

Try this
"car-to-car tracking shot beside a red convertible on a coastal road at sunset, the camera matching its speed"
