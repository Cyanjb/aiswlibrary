Caravaggio

What it means

(1571-1610) Italian painter of dramatic scenes where figures burst out of pitch-black backgrounds in a harsh beam of light. Real, gritty faces and theatrical moments.

When to use it

Use for intense, dramatic scenes and moody portraits. It is very dark, so it is wrong for bright or cheerful images.
