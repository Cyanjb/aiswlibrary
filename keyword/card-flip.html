Card Flip

What it means

A card that turns over like a playing card when you click or hover, showing more information on its back. It flips back to the front when you are done.

When to use it

Use it to tuck a short description or a button behind a picture, like on flashcards or product tiles. Keep the back short, since flipping hides the front.
