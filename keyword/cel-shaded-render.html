Cel-Shaded Render

What it means

3D shapes painted with flat blocks of color and bold outlines, so they look like a cartoon or anime drawing.

When to use it

Use when you want 3D depth with a hand-drawn feel, great for characters and game scenes. Fine painterly detail gets flattened.
