Cel Shading

What it means

Flat blocks of color with crisp, hard-edged shadows, like a frame from a hand-painted cartoon. No soft blending, just clean light and dark.

When to use it

Use when you want a clean animated look for characters, games or scenes. It flattens fine texture, so skip it if you want painterly detail.
