Pottery and Ceramics

Celadon

What it means

A soft jade-green glaze, glassy and pale, used on Chinese and Korean pottery for over a thousand years.

When to use it

Use for serene, elegant pottery and calm still lifes. The green is gentle, so avoid busy colors around it.
