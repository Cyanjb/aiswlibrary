Character Sheet

What it means

One character shown several times on a single page, usually front, side and back, sometimes with faces and outfits too.

When to use it

Use when you want to design a character and keep them consistent later. Small details can change between poses, so check each one.

Try this
"character sheet of a young witch, front side and back views"

illustration · cartoon · line drawing
