Charcoal and Gold Leaf

What it means

Smudgy black charcoal drawing with shining patches of real-looking gold leaf. Moody and precious at once.

When to use it

Use for elegant portraits, animals and sacred-feeling art. Gold can look flat yellow, so ask for 'metallic gold leaf' to keep the shine.

Try this
"charcoal sketch of a horse with metallic gold leaf mane"
