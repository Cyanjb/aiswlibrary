Chartreuse

What it means

A sharp yellow-green, halfway between lime and lemon, named after a French herbal liqueur. Bold, zesty and a little bit odd.

When to use it

Use when you want a fresh, eye-grabbing color that feels fashion-forward. Too much can look sickly, so use it as an accent.
