Camera Movements

Chase One-Take

What it means

A runner chased down a wet street at night, the camera running ahead, then swinging round to follow.

When to use it

Use it for chases and pursuits. Keep it to one runner and a few simple chasers, because crowds tend to fall apart in a long take.

Try this
"the camera leads a runner down a wet night street, then swings round to follow as police close in"
