Cheese Pull

What it means

Long, stretchy strings of melted cheese pulling apart as a slice or sandwich is lifted. Gooey and impossible to ignore.

When to use it

Use for pizza, grilled cheese and lasagna shots that need instant appetite. Strings can come out looking like rubber, so add words like melted and gooey.

Try this
"lifting a slice of pizza, long cheese pull, warm light"
Pairs well with

wood-fired pizza · slow motion · close-up
