Chinese Ink Wash Painting

What it means

Black ink brushed onto paper or silk, from deep strokes to pale grey washes. Misty mountains, bamboo, and lots of quiet empty space.

When to use it

Use for calm, poetic landscapes and nature studies. It's mostly black and grey, so add 'light color' if you want a touch of tint.
