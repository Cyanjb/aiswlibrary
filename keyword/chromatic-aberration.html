Chromatic Aberration

What it means

Thin red and blue color fringes along the edges of things, like an old or cheap lens splitting the light. It gives a slightly glitchy, retro camera feel.

When to use it

Use for edgy, retro, dreamlike or sci-fi looks. A little goes far; too much just looks blurry.
