Claymorphism UI

What it means

Soft, puffy buttons and cards that look shaped from colorful modeling clay, with rounded edges and gentle shadows.

When to use it

Use for kids' apps, games, friendly money apps and playful product pages. Can look toy-like for serious or luxury brands.
