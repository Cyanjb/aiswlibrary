Science and Elements

Cloud Chamber

What it means

A dark, misty box where invisible particles from space leave thin, wispy trails that appear and fade, like tiny shooting stars.

When to use it

Use for mysterious science scenes and abstract, starry textures. It is a niche idea, so describe the wispy trails in your prompt too.

Try this
"cloud chamber, thin glowing particle trails in mist, black background"
Pairs well with

long exposure · macro photography · fog
