Cobalt Blue

What it means

A strong, bright, pure blue, deeper than sky blue and brighter than navy. Think of blue glass bottles and painted pottery.

When to use it

Use when you want a bold blue that feels clean and confident. It can overpower a scene, so use it for one star element.
