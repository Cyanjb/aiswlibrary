Coco Chanel

What it means

French designer Coco Chanel (1883-1971) freed women from corsets with comfortable jersey, collarless tweed suits trimmed in braid, the little black dress and long ropes of pearls.

When to use it

Use for chic, timeless Parisian elegance in black, white and cream. It points to her classic look, so describe the outfit if you want something modern.
