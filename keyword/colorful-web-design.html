Web and App Design Styles

Colorful Web Design

What it means

Bright, high-contrast colors and gradients (smooth blends from one color to another) across buttons, cards and backgrounds.

When to use it

Use for creative apps, events, kids' brands and anything that should feel fun and memorable. Easy to overdo, so keep text areas calm and readable.
