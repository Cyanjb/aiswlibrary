Colour Separation

What it means

Your subject's colour stands apart from the colours around it.

When to use it

Use it to make the subject pop without any lighting tricks. Warm against cool is the easiest pair to ask for.

Try this
"colour separation, a robot in warm orange tones against a cool blue alley"

teal and orange · rim light · leading lines
