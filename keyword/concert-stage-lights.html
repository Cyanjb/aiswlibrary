Concert Stage Lights

What it means

Bright beams of colored light cutting through haze above a stage and a crowd. Big, loud energy you can see.

When to use it

Use for gigs, festivals and big hero moments. Haze or fog is what makes the beams show, so add it in.

Try this
"rock band on stage, colored concert stage lights through haze, crowd silhouettes"
