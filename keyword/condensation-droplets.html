Condensation Droplets

What it means

Tiny beads of water clinging to the outside of a cold glass or bottle. They make a drink look ice-cold and refreshing.

When to use it

Use for cold drinks, iced coffee and summer scenes. Close shots with backlight make the droplets sparkle.
