Contemporary Dance

What it means

Free, flowing movement that mixes reaching, falling and rolling, often barefoot in simple clothes. Expressive and emotional.

When to use it

Use for feelings that are hard to say: grief, joy, longing. Keep backgrounds simple so the body tells the story.
