Conversion Landing Page Design

What it means

A simple, no-distraction page that leads visitors step by step toward one action, like signing up or buying, with clear headlines, reviews and big buttons. Designers call that action a conversion.

When to use it

Use for sales pages, sign-up pages and product launches. It is built to sell, so it can feel pushy for a portfolio or story site.
