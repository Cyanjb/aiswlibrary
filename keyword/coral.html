Basic Color Spectrum

Coral

What it means

A lively pinkish orange, warm and happy, like a summer sunset or tropical flowers.

When to use it

Use for cheerful, beachy and summery images. It can look loud next to other bright colors, so pair it with white, navy or teal.

"coral beach hut against a turquoise sea"
