Style Suffixes

-core

What it means

Add -core to almost any word and it becomes a whole mood: cottagecore, goblincore, normcore. It tells the AI to build a world of objects, colors and clothing around that one idea.

When to use it

Use to turn a single theme into a full aesthetic. Brand-new combos may be too new for the AI to know, so add a few details too.

Try this
"mushroomcore reading nook, moss, soft window light"
Pairs well with

pixiecore · moss covered · golden hour

Works in

midjourney · nano-banana-pro · gpt-image-2
