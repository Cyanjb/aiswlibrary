Corporate Headshot

What it means

A clean, friendly head and shoulders portrait on a plain or softly blurred background, with even, flattering light and a confident smile.

When to use it

Use for team pages, profile pictures and speaker bios. Add an age, outfit and background color so every headshot doesn't look the same.

Try this
"corporate headshot of a woman in a grey blazer, soft grey backdrop, friendly smile"
