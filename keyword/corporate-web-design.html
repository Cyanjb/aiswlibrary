Corporate Web Design

What it means

A professional, orderly site with structured grids, simple layouts and consistent Open Sans and Poppins type, like you see on big company sites.

When to use it

Use for law firms, consultants, finance and companies that sell to other businesses. Can feel stiff and impersonal for creative brands.

Try this
"website for an accounting firm, corporate web design, navy and white, tidy grid"
