Cottage Dress

What it means

A soft, romantic dress with puffed sleeves, a fitted bodice and tiny floral print, often in cotton or linen. Feels like picking wildflowers and baking bread.

When to use it

Use for countryside, picnic and garden scenes with a gentle, storybook mood. Pairs naturally with baskets, meadows and golden light.
