Count Dracula

What it means

The vampire from the original 1897 novel: a tall old man dressed all in black, with a long white moustache, pointed ears, sharp teeth and hair on his palms. He grows younger as he feeds. Describe the book version, not later film versions.

When to use it

Use for gothic horror and creepy castle portraits. Asking for the novel's description gives a stranger, fresher look than the usual caped movie vampire.
