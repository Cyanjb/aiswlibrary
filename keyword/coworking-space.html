Coworking Space

What it means

A shared, stylish workspace where freelancers and small teams rent desks: exposed brick, cozy sofas, phone booths, coffee bar and lots of plants.

When to use it

Use for freelance life, networking and creative work scenes. Name a mood like cozy or busy to set the energy.
