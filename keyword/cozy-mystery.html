Cozy Mystery

What it means

The gentle detective story: a small village, a bookshop or bakery, a cat on the counter and a clue hidden among the teacups. More curious than scary.

When to use it

Use for warm, charming scenes with a hint of intrigue. Not the choice for real danger or gore, that is the whole point.
