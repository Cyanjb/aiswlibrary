Camera Movements

Crash Zoom Out

What it means

A split-second zoom from tight to wide, the opposite of a snap zoom.

When to use it

Use for comedy and shock reveals, like a close-up that crashes out to show the chaos around them. Keep the clip short so the jolt lands.

Try this
"crash zoom out from his calm face to reveal the kitchen on fire around him"
