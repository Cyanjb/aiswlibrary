Motion Graphics Styles

Crayon Picture Book

What it means

A children's book page being drawn as you watch: wax crayon on bumpy paper, color spilling past the lines, every line gently wiggling, and a watercolor wash that sweeps across to reveal hidden white crayon drawings.

When to use it

Perfect for kids' stories, family brands and sweet explainers. It reads as made for children, so it can feel too young for grown-up business topics.
