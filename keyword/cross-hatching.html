Cross-Hatching

What it means

Shading made from layers of crisscrossing pen lines. The more lines stack up, the deeper the shadow.

When to use it

Use for engraved, sketchbook or old-newspaper looks. It can get busy, so pair it with a clear subject.

Try this
"cross-hatching pen drawing of an old sailor, dramatic shadows"
