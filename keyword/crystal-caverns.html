Crystal Caverns

What it means

Huge caves filled with giant glowing crystals in every color, sparkling like the inside of a geode.

When to use it

Use for fantasy quests, magical hideaways and jewel-like scenes. Name a color or two, or it can turn into a rainbow mess.
