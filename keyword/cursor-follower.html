Web Motion and Effects

Cursor Follower

What it means

A soft coloured circle trails behind the mouse pointer with a little delay, like it is chasing it. It grows when the pointer reaches a button or link, then shrinks back.

When to use it

Use it on creative or portfolio sites to add a playful, custom feel. It does nothing on phones and can distract on busy pages, so keep it subtle.

Try this
"Add a soft pink circle that follows my cursor with a slight delay and grows when it is over a button or link"
