Cutaway Diagram

What it means

A drawing where a slice of the outside is removed so you can peek inside, like a ship, a house or a volcano cut in half. Every room or layer is on show at once.

When to use it

Use to show busy little worlds hiding inside something, such as a dollhouse or a submarine. Very detailed scenes can get crowded, so name only a few rooms.
