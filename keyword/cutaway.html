Other

Cutaway

What it means

A view with part of the outside removed so you can see inside, like a house with the front wall missing.

When to use it

Use to show rooms, machines or creatures inside a shell. It can get messy with too many rooms, so name the ones you want.

Try this
"cutaway of a cozy treehouse showing every room"
Pairs well with

diorama · isometric render · exploded view

midjourney · nano-banana-pro · gpt-image-2
