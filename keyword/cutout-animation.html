Cutout Animation

What it means

Characters and scenery made from flat paper pieces, moved around like puppets on a tabletop. You can see the cut edges and the paper grain.

When to use it

Use for crafty, folk-tale or collage-style stories. Movement looks a little stiff, which is part of the charm.
