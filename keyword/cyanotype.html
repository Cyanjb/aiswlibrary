Cyanotype

What it means

A sun print in deep Prussian blue, where objects laid on special paper leave crisp white ghostly shapes.

When to use it

Use for botanical prints, dreamy photos and vintage science looks. Everything turns blue and white, so plan for that.

Try this
"cyanotype of fern leaves and wildflowers, deep blue and white"
