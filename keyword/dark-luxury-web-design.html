Dark Luxury Web Design

What it means

A high-end, mostly black and grey site with tall, bold Oswald headings and a powerful, expensive feel.

When to use it

Use for luxury goods, cars, fitness and premium drinks. Can feel cold or intimidating for friendly brands.

Try this
"website for a luxury watch brand, dark luxury web design, black and grey, tall bold headings"
