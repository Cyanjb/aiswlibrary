Dark Tech Keynote

What it means

A software launch film on a near-black stage with a faint grid and soft cool light. A cursor leads, hundreds of notifications and files pile up in rhythm, freeze in silence, then fly into one perfect grid on a single beat.

When to use it

Made for software launches, AI tool promos and product feature reels. It is sleek and serious, so it can feel cold for friendly or playful brands.
