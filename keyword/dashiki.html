Dashiki

What it means

A loose, colorful pullover tunic from West Africa with a richly embroidered or printed neckline. Comfortable, roomy and full of bold color.

When to use it

Use for portraits, family gatherings and festival scenes. In the 1960s it also became a proud symbol of Black identity in the United States, which can add meaning to your scene.
