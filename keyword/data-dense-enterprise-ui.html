Web and App Design Styles

Data-Dense Enterprise UI

What it means

A tidy, businesslike screen packed with tables, forms and filters, all lined up in a strict, consistent grid. Plain Plus Jakarta Sans type and quiet colors keep lots of information easy to scan.

When to use it

Use for admin panels, back-office tools and apps where people manage lots of records all day. Too busy and serious for a brand or marketing page.
