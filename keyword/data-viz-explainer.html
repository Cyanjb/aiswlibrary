Data Viz Explainer

What it means

Numbers that tell a story: axes draw, bars grow, lines trace and counters tick up to exact values, then everything else dims so the key number stands out.

When to use it

Ideal for reports, business explainers and social posts with one key stat. The numbers must be real and correct, and it can feel dry without a clear story.
