Delftware

What it means

Glossy white Dutch pottery painted in soft cobalt blue: windmills, tulips, ships and little country scenes.

When to use it

Use for charming tabletop scenes, tile patterns and cozy Dutch kitchens. Close cousin of blue and white porcelain, but more folksy and storybook.
