Dieselpunk

What it means

A gritty, alternate past powered by oil and engines, inspired by the 1920s to 1940s. Think riveted airships, smoke and heavy machines.

When to use it

Use for moody adventure and alternate history scenes. It is darker and heavier than steampunk, with less brass and more steel.

Try this
"dieselpunk airship over a smoky industrial city, dusk"
