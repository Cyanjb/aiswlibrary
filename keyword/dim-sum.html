Dim Sum

What it means

Little dumplings and buns tucked into round bamboo steamer baskets, often with tea on the side. Delicate, shiny and inviting.

When to use it

Use for warm family table scenes and close-up dumpling shots. Pair with steam rising so the baskets feel fresh.
