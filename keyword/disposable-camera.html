Disposable Camera

What it means

The look of a cheap throwaway film camera: bright on-camera flash, red eyes, slight blur and grainy colors. Messy, fun and very real.

When to use it

Use for party shots, teen memories and casual 90s or Y2K snapshots. It is meant to look imperfect, so do not expect polish.
