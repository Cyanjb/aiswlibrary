Doodle Web Design

What it means

A hand-drawn site full of little sketches, wobbly lines and handwritten Delius Swash Caps lettering, like notes in a school notebook.

When to use it

Use for kids' brands, teachers, personal blogs and friendly small shops. Can look messy on pages packed with information.
