Doodles on Photo

What it means

A real photo with playful white or colored doodles drawn over it, like hearts, wings, sparkles and little faces.

When to use it

Use for fun social posts, cheeky portraits and pet photos. Keep the doodles simple, busy drawings can bury the photo.

"photo of a corgi with white doodle wings and a crown"
