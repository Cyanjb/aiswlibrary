Dorothea Lange

What it means

(1895-1965) American documentary photographer known for honest, tender portraits of working families during the Great Depression. Plain black and white, weathered faces, quiet strength.

When to use it

Use for serious, human portraits with an old American feel. The style is about dignity, not drama, so keep subjects respectful.
