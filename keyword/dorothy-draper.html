Interior Designers

Dorothy Draper

What it means

American decorator (1889-1969) known for big, happy glamour: black and white checkerboard floors, huge cabbage rose prints, bright pink and green, and giant white plaster scrolls.

When to use it

Use for bold, cheerful hotel lobbies and dramatic rooms. Not for minimal or quiet scenes.
