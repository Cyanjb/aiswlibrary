Double Exposure

What it means

Two pictures blended into one, like a person's silhouette filled with a forest or a city. Dreamy and poetic, as if memories overlap.

When to use it

Use for album covers, posters and emotional portraits. Keep both images simple, or the blend turns to mush.

Try this
"double exposure of a woman's profile and a pine forest"
Pairs well with

silhouette · fog · high-key lighting
