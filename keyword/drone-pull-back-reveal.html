Drone Pull-Back Reveal

What it means

A drone starts close on the subject and flies backward and up, revealing where they really are.

When to use it

Use for big reveals: someone alone on a cliff, a tiny boat on a huge sea, a house in the middle of nowhere. Save it for the last shot of a scene.

Try this
"drone pull-back reveal from a hiker on a rock, flying back and up to show the endless canyon around her"
Pairs well with

aerial shot · dolly out · extreme wide shot
