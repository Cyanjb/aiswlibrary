Dry Brush

What it means

A nearly dry brush dragged across paper or canvas, so the paint skips and leaves scratchy, broken streaks. Rough, airy and full of texture.

When to use it

Use for wind, grass, weathered wood and rugged landscapes. It's less suited to smooth skin or glossy surfaces.
