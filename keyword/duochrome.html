Advanced Color Spectrum

Duochrome

What it means

A finish that shifts between two colors as you move, like a beetle shell or shimmery eyeshadow going from purple to green.

When to use it

Use for cars, makeup, beetles and futuristic fashion. It is related to iridescent, but duochrome sticks to just two colors.
