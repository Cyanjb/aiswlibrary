Dust Particles

What it means

Tiny specks floating in the air, glowing where a beam of light catches them. They make a room feel still, old and lived-in.

When to use it

Use in sunny windows, attics, libraries and quiet moments. They only show up well when there's a strong beam of light to catch them.
