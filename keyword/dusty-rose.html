Basic Color Spectrum

Dusty Rose

What it means

A muted, grayish pink, softer and calmer than bubblegum. Like old roses or faded silk.

When to use it

Use for gentle, romantic and vintage looks. If you want pink that pops, choose a brighter pink instead.
