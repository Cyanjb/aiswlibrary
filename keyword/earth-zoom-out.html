Camera Movements

Earth Zoom Out

What it means

Starts tight on one person, then pulls up and out: the street, the city, the country, until the whole Earth hangs in space.

When to use it

Use to make one small moment feel huge, or to open or close a story about the world. The reverse (space down to one face) is a powerful opener too.

Try this
"earth zoom out from a girl on a rooftop in Cape Town, up through the clouds until the whole planet hangs in space"
Pairs well with

aerial shot · infinite zoom · crane shot
