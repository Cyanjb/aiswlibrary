Editorial HUD

What it means

A precise magazine page come to life: giant narrow headlines, fine grid lines and a running on-screen readout like a timecode and progress bar, on pastel backgrounds. Each cut zooms or wipes through the thing you are already looking at.

When to use it

Great for how-it-works explainers, step-by-step processes and behind-the-scenes reels. It looks smart but busy, so it needs a clear script to stay readable.
