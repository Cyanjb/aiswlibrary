Editorial Poster Web Design

What it means

A warm, confident site that looks like a graphic poster, alternating cream and burnt orange sections with an amber accent and bold Chakra Petch headings.

When to use it

Use for agencies, events, food and lifestyle brands that want graphic punch with warmth. The strong orange may clash with cool brand colors.

"website for a jazz festival, editorial poster web design, cream and burnt orange stripes"
