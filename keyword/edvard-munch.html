Edvard Munch

What it means

(1863-1944) Norwegian painter of The Scream, known for wavy, emotional landscapes and figures soaked in anxious feeling. Blood-orange skies and swirling lines.

When to use it

Use for haunting, emotional, unsettling scenes. It is a heavy mood, not a happy one.
