Fashion Timeline and History

Edwardian Fashion

What it means

Dress from the early 1900s: high lace collars, puffy blouses, long sweeping skirts and enormous hats piled with flowers and feathers. Elegant, layered and very ladylike.

When to use it

Use for garden parties, grand hotels and early 1900s city streets. It can blur into Victorian, so add the decade if you need it exact.
