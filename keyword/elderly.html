Body Types

Elderly

What it means

An older person, often in their seventies or beyond, with lined skin, silver or thinning hair and hands that show a long life. Shown with dignity, warmth and character.

When to use it

Use to bring older people into your scenes as full, active people: gardeners, dancers, teachers, grandparents. Name an activity so they are doing, not just sitting.
