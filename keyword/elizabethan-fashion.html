Fashion Timeline and History

Elizabethan Fashion

What it means

English court dress from the late 1500s: huge starched lace ruffs around the neck, stiff jeweled bodices, wide skirts held out by hoops, and rich brocade sewn with pearls.

When to use it

Use for royal portraits, historical drama and Shakespeare-style scenes. Very ornate, so keep the rest of the scene simple.
