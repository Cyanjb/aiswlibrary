Interior Designers

Elsie de Wolfe

What it means

American decorator (1859-1950) often called the first professional interior designer. She swapped dark Victorian rooms for light walls, mirrors, garden trellis and pretty chintz.

When to use it

Use for airy, feminine, garden-inspired rooms with an early 1900s feel. Keeps things soft and fresh rather than grand.

Try this
"sunroom with green trellis walls and chintz chairs, Elsie de Wolfe style"
