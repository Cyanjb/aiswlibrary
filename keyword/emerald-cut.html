Jewelry and Gemstones

Emerald Cut

What it means

A rectangle-shaped gem cut with clipped corners and long, step-like facets. Instead of sparkle, it gives clean flashes of light like a hall of mirrors.

When to use it

Use for elegant, vintage-feeling rings and pendants, with any stone, not just emeralds. Say the stone and the setting for best results.

Try this
"emerald cut diamond engagement ring, platinum setting, soft window light"
