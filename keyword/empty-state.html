Empty State

What it means

What a page shows when there is nothing in it yet: a friendly little drawing, a short message and a button to get started. It turns a blank space into a clear next step.

When to use it

Use it on any list, inbox or gallery that can be empty, especially for brand new users. Keep the words short and the button obvious.

Try this
"When the saved picks list is empty, show a simple line drawing of an open box with a heart, the words Nothing here yet and an Add first pick button."
