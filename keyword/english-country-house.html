English Country House

What it means

Faded chintz sofas, floral curtains, worn rugs, dogs by the fire, oil portraits and piles of books. Comfortable, a bit shabby and full of history.

When to use it

Use for cozy old manor rooms that feel lived in for generations. Best when things look slightly worn rather than brand new.
