Epic Fantasy Map

What it means

The hand-drawn world map at the front of a fantasy novel: mountains drawn as little peaks, winding rivers, sea monsters in the corners and old-style lettering.

When to use it

Use for worldbuilding, book covers and game art. Place names will often be invented gibberish, so ask for few labels or none.

Try this
"epic fantasy map of an island kingdom, ink on parchment, sea serpents"
