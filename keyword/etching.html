Printing and Transfer Techniques

Etching

What it means

A print made from lines scratched into a metal plate and bitten with acid, so the picture is built from fine crosshatched lines.

When to use it

Use for old book illustrations, maps and detailed vintage scenes. Mostly black and white, with tiny delicate lines.

Try this
"etching of an old harbor town, fine crosshatching, sepia ink"
