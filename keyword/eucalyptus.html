Eucalyptus

What it means

Round, coin-shaped or long leaves in a soft silvery blue-green, often tied in loose bunches. Fresh and calm.

When to use it

Use for wedding florals, spa-like scenes and gentle green palettes. A few sprigs soften almost any still life.
