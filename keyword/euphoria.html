Euphoria

What it means

Pure, bursting joy: arms thrown wide, heads tipped back laughing, color and light everywhere. The kind of moment you want to live in.

When to use it

Use for celebrations, music, festivals and big wins. It can tip into chaos, so give it one clear subject to hold onto.
