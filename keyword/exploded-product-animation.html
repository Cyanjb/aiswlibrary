Exploded Product Animation

What it means

A clean tech reveal where a product separates layer by layer and floats apart in a calm studio. The camera glides between the pieces, thin lines link each part to a label, then it all clicks back together.

When to use it

Ideal for gadget launches, how-it's-made explainers and spec breakdowns. It needs a product with real layers inside, so it does little for soft or simple items.

Try this
"12-second gadget reveal, exploded product animation, headphones separating into layers with white label lines, then clicking back together"
