Shot Sizes and Angles

Eye Level

What it means

The camera sits at the subject's eye height, looking straight at them.

When to use it

Use when you want the angle to feel neutral and honest.

Try this
"eye-level shot of her across the kitchen table"

low-angle · high-angle
