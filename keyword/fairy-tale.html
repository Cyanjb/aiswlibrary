Fairy Tale

What it means

The old storybook world of enchanted woods, tiny cottages, talking animals and castles on hills. Soft, a little dark, and full of once upon a time.

When to use it

Use for magical children's book scenes and dreamy portraits. Add 'dark' if you want the older, stranger versions of these tales.
