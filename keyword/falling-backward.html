Falling Backward

What it means

A body tipping back into the air, arms loose, as if falling into water, a bed of flowers or a dream. It feels like letting go.

When to use it

Use for surreal, dreamy and trust-falling moments. Show what they are falling into, so it reads as peaceful rather than scary.
