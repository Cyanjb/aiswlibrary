Fast Orbit Into Crash Zoom

What it means

A tight, quick circle around the subject that makes it seem to spin, ending in a hard zoom.

When to use it

Use it for spins, a hero turning, or a car drifting. Fast circles can warp faces and hands, so check the result closely.

Try this
"a fast, tight 280 degree orbit around a dancer mid-spin, ending in a crash zoom onto her face"
