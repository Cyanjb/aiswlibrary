Felt and Fabric

What it means

A cozy world made of felt shapes with fuzzy edges and hand stitching on a fabric backdrop. Stitches sew themselves on and each piece hops into place in jerky stop motion, like a hand nudged it between frames.

When to use it

Perfect for warm, friendly explainers, kids' content and small handmade brands. The charm comes from its slow, homemade feel, so it is a poor fit for sleek tech or luxury.
