Femme Fatale

What it means

The glamorous, mysterious woman of old crime films, calm and clever, whose charm hides danger. Think sharp tailoring, window-blind shadows and a knowing look.

When to use it

Use for noir scenes and moody, stylish character portraits. It's a well-worn type, so give her a goal or a job to make her more than a cliche.

Try this
"femme fatale in a trench coat, rainy street, noir lighting"
