Field Guide Page

What it means

A page from a naturalist's guidebook, with neat painted birds, bugs or mushrooms, each with a small label and notes. It feels patient, curious and outdoorsy.

When to use it

Use when you want a collection of creatures or plants shown side by side. Great for made-up species too, if you want a gentle fantasy feel.
