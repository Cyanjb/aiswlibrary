Figure-Ground Illusion

What it means

A picture you can read two ways, like a white vase that is also two dark faces looking at each other.

When to use it

Use for clever posters, logos and dreamy portraits. It works best with two flat colors and simple shapes.

Try this
"figure-ground illusion, two faces forming a vase, black and white"

silhouette · hidden image · hard edge painting
