Jewelry and Gemstones

Filigree

What it means

Jewelry made from tiny twisted threads of gold or silver, curled into delicate lace-like patterns full of little open spaces.

When to use it

Use for heirloom, fantasy and wedding jewelry with lots of fine detail. Works best in close-up; very small filigree can turn to mush.

Try this
"silver filigree earrings with tiny swirls and loops, close-up on linen"
