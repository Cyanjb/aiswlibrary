Filter Chips

What it means

A row of small rounded buttons, called chips, that you tap to narrow down what is shown. Picked chips fill with colour and the grid below rearranges to match.

When to use it

Use it to let people sort a gallery or shop by type in one tap. Too many chips in a row becomes hard to scan, so keep it to the main groups.
