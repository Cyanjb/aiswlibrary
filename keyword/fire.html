Fire

What it means

Flickering orange and yellow flames that glow, dance and throw warm light on everything near them.

When to use it

Use for warmth, danger or drama, from a cozy hearth to a wild blaze. It also works as a strong light source in dark scenes.

"campfire in a dark forest, flames lighting the faces around it"
