Flamenco

What it means

A fiery Spanish dance with swirling ruffled skirts, stamping heels, proud posture and sharp hand movements. Passion in motion.

When to use it

Use for dramatic, colorful scenes with lots of movement and feeling. The flying skirt looks amazing in video.
