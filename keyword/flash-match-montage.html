Flash Match Montage

What it means

A flash of light opens a quick chain of cuts, with a round shape in the same spot each time.

When to use it

Use it to open a montage or show a memory. Pick one simple shape, like a moon, a clock or a plate, so the matches line up.

Try this
"a flash fills the frame, then a chain of match cuts, a full moon, a clock face, a dinner plate, each the same size and position, ending on a kitchen at dawn"
