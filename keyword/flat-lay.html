Flat Lay

What it means

A photo taken straight down at things arranged on a table or floor. Casual, styled and very social media.

When to use it

Use for food, fashion outfits, stationery and product moods. Unlike knolling, it can be loose and artful rather than strictly lined up.

"flat lay of a cozy breakfast, linen napkin, morning light"
