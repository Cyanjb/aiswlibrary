Web and App Design Styles

Flat Web Design

What it means

Everything is flat and two-dimensional: no shadows, no shiny effects, no textures, just bright solid colors and clean Inter type.

When to use it

Use for apps, icons, explainers and fast, simple sites. Can feel a little generic without strong illustrations.
