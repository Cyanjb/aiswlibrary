Flowchart

What it means

Boxes and arrows that walk you through steps or choices, one after another. Clear, friendly and easy to follow at a glance.

When to use it

Use for how-to graphics, explainers and playful decision charts. Keep the words short, because AI often scrambles long labels.

Try this
"hand-drawn flowchart for choosing a houseplant, pastel boxes and arrows"
