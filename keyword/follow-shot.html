Camera Movements

Follow Shot

What it means

The camera follows behind someone as they move.

When to use it

Use to take the viewer on the journey with your character.

Try this
"follow shot behind her as she walks into the crowd"
Pairs well with

tracking-shot · steadicam

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
