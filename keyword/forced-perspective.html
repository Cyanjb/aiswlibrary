Forced Perspective

What it means

A trick where something near the camera lines up with something far away, so a person seems to pinch the moon or hold up a tower.

When to use it

Use for playful travel shots and giant or tiny effects. It needs a clear near subject and far subject, so describe both.

Try this
"forced perspective, girl pinching the full moon between her fingers"
Pairs well with

wide angle lens · deep focus · miniature
