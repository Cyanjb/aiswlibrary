Transitions and Editing

Foreground Wipe

What it means

Something passes right in front of the lens and, as it clears, the scene behind has changed.

When to use it

Use for a smooth one-take feel. One of the easiest transitions to get right in AI video.

Try this
"a blurred figure walks across the lens; as they clear frame we are in a new street"
Pairs well with

match-cut · whip-pan

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
