Frame Within a Frame

What it means

Using a doorway, arch, window or branches to make a natural frame around the subject inside the picture.

When to use it

Use to pull the eye straight to your subject and add depth. Name the frame, like framed by a stone archway.

Try this
"lighthouse framed by a stone archway, golden hour"

deep focus · golden hour · wide shot
