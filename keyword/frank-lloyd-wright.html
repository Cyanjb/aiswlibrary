Architects

Frank Lloyd Wright

What it means

American architect (1867-1959) whose houses hug the land with long, low roofs, warm wood, stone and bands of windows. His most famous home sits right over a waterfall.

When to use it

Use for calm, earthy homes that feel grown from the landscape. Works best for houses and cabins, less so for city towers.

Try this
"house built over a forest waterfall, Frank Lloyd Wright style, autumn"
