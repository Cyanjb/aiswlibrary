Frankenstein's Creature

What it means

The being from the 1818 novel: very tall, with yellowish skin, flowing black hair, watery eyes and dark lips, gentle and well-spoken at first. No neck bolts or flat head, those came from later films. Describe the book version.

When to use it

Use for tragic, thoughtful gothic portraits that feel more human than monster. Say 'as described in the 1818 novel' to steer away from the movie look.
