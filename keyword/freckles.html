Freckles

What it means

Small, scattered brown spots across the nose, cheeks and shoulders. They add warmth and personality to a face.

When to use it

Use to make portraits feel natural and less airbrushed. Say 'light freckles' if they come out too heavy.

"portrait of a smiling teenager with freckles, soft window light"
