Photography Special Effects

Freelensing

What it means

Taking the lens off the camera and holding it loosely in front. Only a thin slice stays sharp, and the rest melts into blur and glowing haze.

When to use it

Use for soft, romantic portraits and flowers. It is unpredictable on purpose, so try a few versions.

Try this
"freelensing portrait of a bride in a meadow, thin slice of focus, hazy light"
