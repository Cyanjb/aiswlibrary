Photo Angles

From Behind

What it means

The subject seen from the back, looking out at what they see. It invites you to stand in their shoes.

When to use it

Use for travel, wonder and quiet, thoughtful moods. Faces stay hidden, which is handy when you do not want a face at all.

Try this
"woman seen from behind looking over a misty valley, fog"
Pairs well with

fog · silhouette · extreme wide shot
