Frutiger Aero

What it means

The hopeful tech look of the late 2000s: sky-blue gradients, glossy see-through bubbles, water droplets, fresh green leaves and shiny aqua buttons. Bubbles float up, wobble and pop to reveal text or icons.

When to use it

Lovely for nostalgic, feel-good tech reels, wellness apps and eco brands. Its sunny optimism can feel dated or sugary if your message is serious.
