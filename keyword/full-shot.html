Shot Sizes and Angles

Full Shot

What it means

The whole person from head to feet, nothing cut off.

When to use it

Use to show an outfit, a pose, a dance move or a full action.

Try this
"full shot of the dancer mid-leap"
Pairs well with

medium-shot · wide-shot

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
