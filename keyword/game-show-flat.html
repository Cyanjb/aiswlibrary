Motion Graphics Styles

Game Show Flat

What it means

A sunny daytime TV quiz: flat shapes with thick outlines and hard shadows on striped and sunburst sets. Every pop, tick, buzz and ding lands on one fast beat, with quick cuts and a snap zoom on the big reaction.

When to use it

Made for quizzes, polls, myth versus fact reels and audience questions. It is loud and fast, so it is wrong for gentle or emotional topics.

"15-second quiz reel, game show flat animation, answer tiles popping in on a sunburst set, buzzer and snap zoom on the reveal"
