Style Suffixes

-gaze

What it means

Taken from shoegaze, a hazy, dreamy style of rock music. Adding -gaze suggests soft blur, washed-out color and a floaty, half-remembered feeling.

When to use it

Use for dreamy, out-of-focus moods and album-cover style art. It blurs things on purpose, so it's not for crisp detail.

Try this
"dreamgaze field at dusk, soft blur, faded pink light"
