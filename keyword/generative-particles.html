Generative Particles

What it means

Art made by rules: thousands of tiny particles drift on invisible currents leaving fine trails, then gather into a word or shape on the beat, hold for a breath and dissolve back into the flow.

When to use it

Great for tech brands, music visuals, logo reveals and calm backgrounds. It is abstract, so it will not explain a step-by-step idea on its own.

Try this
"10-second logo reveal, generative particles animation, thousands of glowing particles flowing then gathering into the logo"
