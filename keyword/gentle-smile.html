Gentle Smile

What it means

A soft, closed-lip or barely open smile. Calm and kind, rather than a big toothy grin.

When to use it

Use when a big grin feels fake or too much. Lovely for portraits, book covers and quiet moments.

"older man with a gentle smile, holding a teacup, warm light"
