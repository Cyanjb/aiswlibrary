Patterns

Gingham

What it means

An even checkered pattern made by crossing stripes of one color with white. Think picnic blankets and summer dresses.

When to use it

Use for sweet, homey, countryside feels. It reads as cute and simple, so it is not the choice for edgy or luxury moods.

Try this
"picnic basket on a red gingham blanket, summer meadow"
