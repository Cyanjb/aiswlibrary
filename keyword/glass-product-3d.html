Glass Product 3D

What it means

A see-through glass object floating in an endless black studio, outlined only by long strip lights that leave rainbow edges. You can see its insides, light pulses run through it on the beat, and it slowly comes apart, then snaps shut on the drop.

When to use it

Great for sleek tech teasers, launch trailers and music-synced reveals. It looks premium but cold, so it is not for cozy or handmade brands.
