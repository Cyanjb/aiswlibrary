Web and App Design Styles

Glassmorphism UI

What it means

Cards and panels that look like frosted glass: see-through, softly blurred, with thin glowing edges floating over colorful backgrounds.

When to use it

Use for app screens, music players, weather apps and modern landing pages. Text on glass can be hard to read, so keep enough contrast.
