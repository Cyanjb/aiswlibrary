Food and Cooking Effects

Glossy Glaze

What it means

A shiny, mirror-like coating on food, like a donut glaze, a caramel layer or a sticky roast. It gleams with highlights.

When to use it

Use when food needs to look rich, fresh and expensive. Too much shine can look plastic, so pair it with natural light.
