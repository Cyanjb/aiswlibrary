Gold Leaf

What it means

Paper-thin sheets of real gold pressed onto a surface, so parts of the art shine like metal. You see it in old religious icons, illuminated manuscripts and lacquerware.

When to use it

Use for a precious, glowing accent on halos, borders or backgrounds. A little goes a long way; too much looks flat and brassy.
