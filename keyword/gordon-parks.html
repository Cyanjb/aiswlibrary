Gordon Parks

What it means

(1912-2006) American photographer, filmmaker and writer who captured Black American life and the civil rights era for Life magazine. Warm, dignified portraits in rich color and black and white.

When to use it

Use for heartfelt mid-century portraits and everyday family scenes with real warmth. Keep the people at the center and treat them with respect.
