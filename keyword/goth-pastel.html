Motion Graphics Styles

Goth Pastel

What it means

A black, deadpan, totally still frame with one unimpressed line of text, then a sudden explosion of candy rainbow gradients, sparkles, hearts and glossy stickers, then instantly back to stillness as if nothing happened.

When to use it

Perfect for funny reels, ironic ads and personal brands with a dry sense of humor. The joke only works with good timing, and it can feel random without a punchline.

Try this
"10-second comedy reel, goth pastel animation, still black frame with deadpan text, then a burst of rainbow stickers and sparkles"
