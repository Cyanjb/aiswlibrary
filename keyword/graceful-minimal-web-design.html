Graceful Minimal Web Design

What it means

A graceful, refined site with delicate thin type, a minimal palette and polished layouts that feel quietly sophisticated.

When to use it

Use for designers, boutiques, galleries and premium services. The delicate type can get lost on busy photos or small screens.

Try this
