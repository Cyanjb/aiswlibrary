Web and App Design Styles

Gradient Web Design

What it means

Smooth blends of color washing across backgrounds, buttons and shapes, with Montserrat and Space Grotesk type for a modern, playful sense of depth.

When to use it

Use for apps, music, startups and social brands that want a fresh, glowing look. Too many gradients at once can look muddy.
