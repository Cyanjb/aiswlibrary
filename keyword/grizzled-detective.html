Cliche Roles

Grizzled Detective

What it means

A tired, weather-beaten investigator with stubble, a rumpled coat and a long memory. They've seen too much and keep going anyway.

When to use it

Use for crime stories, rainy cities and late-night offices. Add a city and an era to keep it from looking like every other noir.

Try this
"grizzled detective under a streetlamp, rain, neon reflections"
