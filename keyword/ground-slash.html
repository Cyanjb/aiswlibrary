Camera Movements

Ground Slash

What it means

The camera skims just above the ground beside the subject and snaps its aim hard, up and back down.

When to use it

Use it for low, aggressive sport or skate footage. Two snaps is plenty; more and it turns to mush.

Try this
"the camera skims just above the ground beside a skater, with two hard snaps of the aim from feet to head and back"
Pairs well with

ground level · tracking shot · handheld
