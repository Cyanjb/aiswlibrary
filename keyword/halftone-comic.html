Motion Graphics Styles

Halftone Comic

What it means

A vintage comic page come to life: thick black outlines, flat colors filled with printed dots, panels that wipe in one after another, and impact bursts that shake the frame.

When to use it

Fun for punchy reels, product launches and story recaps with big moments. Busy pages can crowd out text, so keep each panel to one idea.
