A retro printed case file: cream paper, halftone dots in a few flat inks, huge headlines with misprinted shadows, rubber stamps and redaction bars.

When to use it

Perfect for true crime, mysteries, conspiracy teasers and "the files" style storytelling. It can feel serious, so it suits real investigations or playful fake ones.
