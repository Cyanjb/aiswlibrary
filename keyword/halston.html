Halston

What it means

American designer Halston (1932-1990) defined 1970s glamour with simple, fluid pieces: jersey halter gowns, caftans, wrap shapes and clean minimal lines that moved on the dance floor.

When to use it

Use for sleek 1970s nightlife and minimalist evening wear. Great with disco light, but keep the clothes simple to stay true to him.
