UI Components

Hamburger Menu

What it means

An icon of three stacked lines that opens a hidden menu when tapped. Here the lines morph into an X while the menu slides down, so you know how to close it.

When to use it

Use it on small screens to tuck navigation away and keep the page clean. Hidden menus get used less, so keep your most important link visible too.
