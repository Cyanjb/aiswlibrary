Worldwide Fashion

Hanbok

What it means

Traditional Korean clothing. For women, a short jacket called a jeogori over a full, high-waisted skirt called a chima; for men, a jacket with roomy trousers. Soft colors and graceful lines.

When to use it

Use for Korean family celebrations and portraits. Hanbok is worn for special days like Seollal, Chuseok, a baby's first birthday and weddings, so show it with that care.
