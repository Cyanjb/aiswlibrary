Transitions and Editing

Hard Cut

What it means

The plain, everyday join: one shot simply ends and the next one begins.

When to use it

Use it when nothing fancier earns its place, which is most of the time. It is added in editing, not in the prompt: make two clips and join them.

Try this
"in the edit, place clip B straight after clip A, with the runner moving left to right in both"

match cut · jump cut · smash cut
