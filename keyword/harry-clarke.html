Illustrators

Harry Clarke

What it means

(1889-1931) Irish stained glass artist and illustrator of eerie fairy tales and Edgar Allan Poe stories. Super fine ink detail, jewel patterns and pale, haunting faces.

When to use it

Use for dark, ornate, spooky illustrations full of tiny detail. Great for gothic stories, less so for cheerful ones.
