HD-2D

What it means

Pixel-art characters standing like paper cutouts inside a softly lit 3D miniature world, with glowing light, fog and dreamy blur at the edges.

When to use it

Lovely for game-style stories, fantasy trailers and nostalgic gamer content. It feels like a modern take on a 90s console game.
