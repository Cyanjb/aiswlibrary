Head Dip Match Cut

What it means

The subject dips their head in one place and lifts it in another.

When to use it

Use it for portrait films, or to show a whole life in a few cuts. Keep the framing and size the same in both places.

Try this
"an old fisherman bows his head on a misty pier; as he lifts it he is in a busy city cafe, same framing and size"
