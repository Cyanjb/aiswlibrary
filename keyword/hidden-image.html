Illusions

Hidden Image

What it means

A picture with a second picture tucked inside it, like a forest whose trees form a face when you step back.

When to use it

Use for puzzles, posters and magical double meanings. It is tricky for AI, so name both images clearly and keep the scene simple.

Try this
"autumn forest where the trees form a hidden image of an owl"

figure-ground illusion · pareidolia · surrealist pop
