Hieronymus Bosch

What it means

(c. 1450-1516) Dutch painter of crowded, strange dream worlds full of tiny creatures, odd machines and giant fruit. Funny, spooky and endlessly weird.

When to use it

Use for surreal, busy fantasy landscapes packed with little details. Expect chaos, so it is not for simple, clean images.
