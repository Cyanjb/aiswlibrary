High Selfie Angle

What it means

The classic selfie view, with the camera held above the head and tilted down. Big eyes, small chin and an arm reaching just out of frame.

When to use it

Use for casual phone-style portraits and social media looks. Models may add a stretched arm or a phone, so say no phone visible if you want it hidden.
