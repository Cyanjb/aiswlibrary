Highland Cow

What it means

A shaggy Scottish cow with long ginger hair over its eyes and wide curved horns. Scruffy, gentle and wildly lovable.

When to use it

Use for cozy countryside scenes, cute farm portraits and misty hills. The fringe over the eyes is the charm, so keep the face close.
