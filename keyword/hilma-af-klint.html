Painters

Hilma af Klint

What it means

(1862-1944) Swedish painter who made huge, colorful abstract paintings years before abstract art was famous. Spirals, circles, petals and soft pastel symbols.

When to use it

Use for spiritual, cosmic and geometric abstract art with gentle color. Not for realistic scenes.
