Painters

Hokusai

What it means

(c. 1760-1849) Japanese artist behind The Great Wave, known for woodblock prints with flat bold colors, crisp outlines, deep blues and views of Mount Fuji.

When to use it

Use for Japanese print-style waves, mountains and everyday scenes. It is a flat print look, so skip it for realism.
