Hollywood Regency

What it means

Old Hollywood glamour at home: glossy lacquer, mirrored furniture, gold accents, bold black and white, and jewel-colored velvet.

When to use it

Use for glamorous, a little theatrical rooms with movie star energy. Too much gold can look cheap, so balance it with black or white.
