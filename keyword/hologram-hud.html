Motion Graphics Styles

Hologram HUD

What it means

A gadget is scanned into a glowing wireframe above a round projector. A target box snaps onto one part, its shell slides open, a line pulls out and scrambled symbols roll into the real number.

When to use it

Great for tech product reveals, sci-fi trailers and spec breakdowns. It needs a real object with parts worth pointing at, and too many labels quickly crowd the frame.
