Holographic Shimmer

What it means

A rainbow sheen that shifts across a surface, like a hologram sticker or a soap bubble. Colors slide from pink to blue to gold as the light moves.

When to use it

Use for futuristic fashion, packaging, stickers and dreamy objects. It's a bit loud, so put it on one thing rather than the whole scene.
