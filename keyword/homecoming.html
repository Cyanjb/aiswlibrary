Storytelling

Homecoming

What it means

A traveler arriving back where they started, changed by the journey. Porch lights on, a dog running out, a familiar door seen with new eyes.

When to use it

Use for warm, emotional endings and reunions. Golden light and a slightly wider shot help it feel like the end of a long story.
