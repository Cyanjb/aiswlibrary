Houndstooth

What it means

A broken check made of little jagged four-pointed shapes, usually black and white. Classic, sharp and a little bit British tailoring.

When to use it

Use for fashion, coats and smart retro looks. Large areas of it can shimmer or warp, so keep it on clothing or trim.
