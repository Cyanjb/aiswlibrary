Web Motion and Effects

Hover Lift

What it means

When the mouse passes over a card, it rises a little and casts a deeper shadow, then settles back down when the mouse leaves. It tells you the card can be clicked.

When to use it

Use it on grids of clickable cards, like products, trips or articles. Phones have no hover, so make sure the cards still look tappable without it.

Try this
"Make each card lift up with a deeper shadow when I hover over it, and settle back when I move away"
