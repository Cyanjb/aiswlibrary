Hyper Motion Product

What it means

A high-speed close-up commercial where flames lick, splashes freeze mid-air and crumbs hang in slow motion, then everything snaps to full speed on the beat and the product lands center frame with its name slammed on top.

When to use it

Made for food, drink and product ads that need to feel exciting. It depends on great close-up footage or renders, and it is overkill for quiet or talky content.

Try this
"8-second snack ad, hyper motion product shot, crumbs hanging in slow motion then snapping to full speed as the bag lands"
