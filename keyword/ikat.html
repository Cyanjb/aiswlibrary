Ikat

What it means

A woven pattern with soft, feathery edges, because the threads are dyed in sections before weaving. It is a centuries-old craft from places like Indonesia, Central Asia and India.

When to use it

Use for warm, handmade textiles with a blurred, dreamy edge. AI often makes it too sharp, so ask for blurred edges and woven texture.

Try this
"indigo ikat cushion on a rattan chair, blurred feathered edges"
