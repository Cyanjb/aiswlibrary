Illuminated Manuscript

What it means

A handwritten page from the Middle Ages, glowing with gold leaf, rich colors and a giant decorated first letter. Vines, tiny animals and little figures crawl around the margins.

When to use it

Use for fantasy, history and anything that should feel sacred or precious. The writing will usually be pretend script, so treat it as decoration.
