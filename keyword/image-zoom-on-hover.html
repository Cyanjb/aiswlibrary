Web Motion and Effects

Image Zoom on Hover

What it means

When the mouse rests on a picture, the image slowly zooms in inside its frame while a caption slides up from the bottom. The frame stays the same size; only the picture grows.

When to use it

Use it on galleries, portfolios and shop items to invite a closer look. Keep the zoom slow and small, or it feels jumpy.

Try this
"When I hover a photo card, slowly zoom the image inside its frame and slide a caption up from the bottom"
