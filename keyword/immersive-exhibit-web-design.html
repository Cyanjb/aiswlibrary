Web and App Design Styles

Immersive Exhibit Web Design

What it means

A site that feels like walking through an interactive museum exhibit, all on one continuous deep green canvas with crisp white type, storytelling, animation and little game-like moments.

When to use it

Use for campaigns, museums, brand stories and product launches people explore once. Slow for visitors who just want quick facts.
