Impasto Oil Animation

What it means

A moving oil painting laid on with a palette knife: big, flat, sharp-edged planes of thick paint, lit from the side so every ridge shows. Only what the story moves moves, like rain, a figure or the light.

When to use it

Great for emotional stories, music videos and artful brand films. Thick paint hides small details, so keep scenes simple and bold.
