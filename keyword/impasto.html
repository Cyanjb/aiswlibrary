Techniques

Impasto

What it means

Paint laid on so thickly that it stands up off the canvas in ridges and peaks. You can almost feel every brushstroke.

When to use it

Use when you want rich, touchable texture and energy. All that surface can fight with fine detail, so keep the subject simple.
