Impossible Staircase

What it means

Stairs that seem to climb forever in a loop, always going up yet ending where they started.

When to use it

Use for puzzling, dreamlike architecture. AI often makes ordinary stairs, so add 'endless looping stairs' and a high angle.

Try this
"impossible staircase looping around a stone tower, high angle"
