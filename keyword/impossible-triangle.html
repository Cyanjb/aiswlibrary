Impossible Triangle

What it means

A solid-looking triangle whose three beams join in a way that could never exist in real space.

When to use it

Use for logos, sculptures and puzzling architecture. AI often fixes it into a normal triangle, so add 'optical illusion' to the prompt.

Try this
"impossible triangle sculpture in a desert, optical illusion"
