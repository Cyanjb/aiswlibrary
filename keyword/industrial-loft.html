Industrial Loft

What it means

Old factory space turned home: exposed brick, steel beams, giant windows, concrete floors and pipes left showing.

When to use it

Use for creative city apartments, studios and moody urban scenes. Can feel cold, so add warm lamps or leather.

Try this
"industrial loft apartment, exposed brick, tall steel windows, evening"
