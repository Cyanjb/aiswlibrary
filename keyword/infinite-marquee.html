Web Motion and Effects

Infinite Marquee

What it means

Rows of big words or little labels glide sideways forever in a smooth endless loop, often with rows moving in opposite directions.

When to use it

Use it for a band of keywords, partner badges or short claims that adds energy to a page. Do not put important information in it, since moving text is hard to read.
