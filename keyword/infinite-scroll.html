Web Motion and Effects

Infinite Scroll

What it means

As you scroll to the bottom of a feed, a small loading sign appears and more items arrive below, so the list never seems to end.

When to use it

Use it for photo feeds, galleries and social style lists that people browse casually. Avoid it when people need to reach a footer or find their place again.

Try this
"Make my gallery load more cards when I scroll to the bottom, with a small spinner while the new ones arrive."
