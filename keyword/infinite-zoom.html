Camera Movements

Infinite Zoom

What it means

The camera keeps zooming into a detail that becomes a whole new scene, then into a detail of that scene, again and again with no cut.

When to use it

Use for dreamlike openings, hidden worlds, and "everything is connected" stories. Plan each layer, because models lose the thread after two or three.

Try this
"infinite zoom into her eye, which becomes a galaxy, which becomes a city at night"
Pairs well with

earth zoom out · push-through · macro lens

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
