Infinity Mirror

What it means

Two mirrors facing each other, making lights or reflections repeat in a tunnel that seems to go on forever.

When to use it

Use for glowing art rooms, dreamy portraits and sci-fi halls. It loves points of light, so add lanterns, LEDs or neon.
