Infographic

What it means

A clean, friendly layout that explains something with icons, simple charts, arrows and short labels. It looks like a poster you would pin up in a classroom.

When to use it

Use when you want to explain a process or show facts at a glance. Keep the words few and short, because AI text can still come out scrambled.
