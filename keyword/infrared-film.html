Infrared Film

What it means

Film that sees invisible light, turning green leaves and grass bright white or candy pink while skies go dark. Familiar places suddenly look like another planet.

When to use it

Use for surreal landscapes and dreamy, otherworldly nature. Say black and white infrared for white trees, or color infrared for pink trees.

Try this
"color infrared photo of a forest lake, pink trees"
