Ink and Watercolor

What it means

Crisp pen lines with soft watercolor washes under them. The lines hold the shape, the paint adds mood.

When to use it

Use when plain watercolor gets too blurry and you want clear detail. Great for travel sketches, botanicals and storybook scenes.
