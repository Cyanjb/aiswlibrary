Ink Wash Animation

What it means

A quiet East Asian ink painting that paints itself: one brush lays tapering strokes, grey washes bloom outward like ink in water, and mountains or birds rise out of mist. Most of the frame stays empty paper and the camera drifts slowly.

When to use it

Beautiful for calm, reflective openers, poetry, tea or wellness brands. It is meant to be slow and spare, so it will not carry a busy, info-heavy video.
