Photography Special Effects

Intentional Camera Movement

What it means

Moving the camera on purpose while the photo is taken, so trees, lights or the sea smear into painterly streaks.

When to use it

Use for abstract, painterly nature and city images. Nothing will be sharp, and that is the point.

Try this
"intentional camera movement photo of a birch forest, vertical streaks"
Pairs well with

long exposure · muted palette · golden autumn
