Irving Penn

What it means

(1917-2009) American photographer known for elegant fashion portraits and still lifes against plain gray backdrops. Calm, precise and beautifully lit, like a museum piece.

When to use it

Use for clean studio portraits, fashion and simple, perfect still lifes. It is a studio look, so it will not help outdoor scenes.
