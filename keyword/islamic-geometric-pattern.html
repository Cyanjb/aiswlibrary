Islamic Geometric Pattern

What it means

Interlocking stars, polygons and rosettes that repeat endlessly, found in tilework, carved wood and plaster across the Islamic world. Precise, balanced and mesmerizing.

When to use it

Use for tiles, ceilings, doorways and decorative backgrounds. AI can get the geometry slightly off, so expect to pick the cleanest result.
