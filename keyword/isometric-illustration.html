Isometric Illustration

What it means

A tidy, angled view where everything is seen from above at the same slant, with nothing shrinking into the distance. Like a tiny diorama or a game map.

When to use it

Use for cute room cutaways, maps and miniature worlds. It doesn't do dramatic perspective, so leave camera angle words out.

Try this
"isometric illustration of a cozy bookshop interior, soft colors"

microscape · low poly art · pastel palette
