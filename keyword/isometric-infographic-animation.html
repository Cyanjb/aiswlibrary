Isometric Infographic Animation

What it means

A system diagram come alive: one floating board of flat three-tone blocks seen from an angle, a dashed route leading from station to station, things slicing open to show their insides, and numbers shown as rows of tiny icons.

When to use it

Ideal for explaining how a system, process or business works. It takes planning to lay out the board, so it is too much for a quick one-fact post.

Try this
"30-second process explainer, isometric infographic animation, dashed route drawing between flat blocks, a factory slicing open to show inside"
