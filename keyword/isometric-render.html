Isometric Render

What it means

A tidy 3D view from above at an angle, with no vanishing point, so it looks like a little game board or dollhouse.

When to use it

Use for cozy rooms, tiny towns and explainer art. The camera angle is fixed, so it is not for dramatic or cinematic shots.

"isometric render of a tiny bakery, warm morning light"
