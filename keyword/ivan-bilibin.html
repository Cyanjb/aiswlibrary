Ivan Bilibin

What it means

(1876-1942) Russian illustrator of folk tales, with bold outlines, flat bright colors and fancy patterned borders inspired by old Russian art.

When to use it

Use for Slavic fairy tales, firebirds and decorated storybook pages. Ask for an ornamental border to get the full page look.
