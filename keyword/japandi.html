Interior Decor

Japandi

What it means

A calm mix of Japanese and Scandinavian rooms: low wood furniture, linen, paper lamps, soft beige and gray, and almost no clutter.

When to use it

Use for peaceful, airy rooms that feel like a deep breath. If you want color or personality, this is the wrong word.
