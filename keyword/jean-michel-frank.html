Interior Designers

Jean-Michel Frank

What it means

French designer (1895-1941) who made quiet luxury: plain rooms in cream and beige, walls of parchment or straw, and chunky simple chairs and tables.

When to use it

Use for soft, minimal, very rich-looking rooms. The magic is in the textures, so name materials like parchment, straw or leather.

Try this
"cream salon with parchment walls and block armchairs, Jean-Michel Frank style"
