Camera Movements

Jib Reveal

What it means

The camera swoops down from above in one arc, glides along the subject and lands on a detail.

When to use it

Use it for product or character intros that finish on one feature. Name the exact detail to land on, or it may stop somewhere random.

Try this
"jib down from above in one arc, glide along a vintage motorbike and settle close on its chrome headlamp"
