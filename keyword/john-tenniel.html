John Tenniel

What it means

(1820-1914) English illustrator who drew the first pictures for Alice's Adventures in Wonderland. Crisp black and white engraved lines and odd, slightly stiff characters.

When to use it

Use for Victorian storybook scenes and curious, Alice-like characters. It is black and white line work, so add color only if you really want it.
