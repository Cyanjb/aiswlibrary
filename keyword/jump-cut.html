Transitions and Editing

Jump Cut

What it means

The camera stays put while time jumps between cuts, so things pop in, out or around.

When to use it

Use for routines, comedy and fast montages.

Try this
"jump cuts on a locked frame as more friends appear beside her"
Pairs well with

smash-cut · match-cut
