Kaiju Film

What it means

The classic Japanese giant monster movie: a towering creature stomping through a miniature city, tiny tanks and planes, smoke, sparks and screaming crowds.

When to use it

Use for big, playful destruction and a sense of huge scale. Add 'miniature set' if you want the handmade, person-in-a-suit charm of the old films.
