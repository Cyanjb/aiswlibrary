Kente Cloth

What it means

Brightly colored cloth from Ghana, made by the Asante and Ewe peoples by weaving narrow strips on a loom and sewing them together. Bold blocks of gold, green, red and black, each pattern with its own name and meaning.

When to use it

Use for celebrations, portraits and graduation scenes. Kente was once reserved for royalty and is still worn for important ceremonies, so treat it as a meaningful cloth, not a random pattern.
