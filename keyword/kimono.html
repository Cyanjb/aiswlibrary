Worldwide Fashion

Kimono

What it means

Japan's traditional robe: a straight, T-shaped garment wrapped around the body and tied with a wide sash called an obi. Fabrics range from simple cotton to silk painted with flowers and cranes.

When to use it

Use for Japanese gardens, tea ceremonies and festivals. Kimono wrap left side over right; right over left is reserved for dressing the deceased, so check your results.
