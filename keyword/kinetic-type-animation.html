Kinetic Type Animation

What it means

Words are the whole show: each spoken word lands on screen right as it is said, slamming, popping, sliding and stacking into bold layouts. When a block is full it pushes back and the next one slams in front.

When to use it

Great for quotes, voiceover reels, lyric videos and hooks. It needs a clear, well-timed voice track, and long sentences get hard to read.
