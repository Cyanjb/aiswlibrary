Knitting

What it means

Soft loops of yarn growing off two needles, often with a ball of wool rolling nearby. Chunky stitches read as warmth you can almost feel.

When to use it

Use for cozy winter scenes, slow-living moments, or a gentle hands-at-work shot. Close crops work best, since fine stitches can turn to mush in wide shots.
