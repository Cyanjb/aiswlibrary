Knolling

What it means

Objects laid out flat and lined up at neat right angles, every item in its own space. Very tidy and very satisfying.

When to use it

Use for tool kits, gear layouts and 'what's in my bag' images. AI can repeat or merge items, so keep the object list short.

Try this
"knolling of vintage camera gear on kraft paper, top-down"
