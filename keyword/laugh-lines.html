Laugh Lines

What it means

The soft creases around the eyes and mouth that come from years of smiling. They make a face look kind and lived-in.

When to use it

Use for honest, warm portraits of adults, especially older people. AI tends to smooth skin, so this word helps keep real character.

Try this
"close-up of a fisherman with laugh lines, overcast light"

close-up · overcast light · candid
