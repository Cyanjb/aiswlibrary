Basic Color Spectrum

Lavender

What it means

A pale, soft purple, like the flowers of the lavender plant. Gentle, dreamy and a little bit magical.

When to use it

Use for calm, sweet and fairy tale moods. It can wash out in bright light, so add a deeper purple if the image feels faded.
