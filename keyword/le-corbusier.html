Architects

Le Corbusier

What it means

Swiss-French architect (1887-1965) who made clean white boxes lifted on slim pillars, with long strip windows and flat roof gardens. Later work used chunky raw concrete.

When to use it

Use for crisp early modern homes and bold concrete housing blocks. Can look cold, so warm it up with sunlight or plants.
