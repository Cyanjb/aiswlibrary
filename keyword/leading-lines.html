Composition

Leading Lines

What it means

Lines in the scene, like roads, rails or fences, that run toward your subject.

When to use it

Use them to pull the eye straight to what matters. Name the lines in your prompt so the model knows what to build.

Try this
"leading lines, a road and train rails meet at a lone robot on the horizon"
Pairs well with

wide shot · deep focus · 24mm wide lens
