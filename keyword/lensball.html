Lensball

What it means

A clear glass ball held up in front of a scene, showing a tiny upside-down copy of the world inside while the background blurs.

When to use it

Use for magical travel shots, sunsets and landscapes with a twist. Remember the view inside the ball is flipped.

Try this
"lensball held up at sunset over a mountain lake, background bokeh"
