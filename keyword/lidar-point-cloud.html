LiDAR Point Cloud

What it means

A real place shown as millions of glowing measured dots floating in the dark. A scan line sweeps through and the dots light up in layers.

When to use it

Perfect for tech, architecture, maps and "how we measured it" explainers. It looks most convincing when built from real scan data.
