Transitions and Editing

Light Match Cut

What it means

A light flares to white, and the next shot opens on a light in the same spot.

When to use it

Use for time jumps and dreamy montages.

Try this
"the lantern flares to white; the next shot is a street lamp in the same place"

match-cut · flash-to-white
