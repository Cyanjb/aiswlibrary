Photography Special Effects

Light Painting

What it means

Drawing with a moving light in the dark while the camera shutter stays open. You get glowing ribbons, swirls and shapes hanging in the air.

When to use it

Use for night scenes with trails of light, sparkler hearts and glowing loops around people. Words written in light often come out misspelled.

"person drawing glowing spirals with a flashlight on a dark beach, light painting"
