Special Effects

Light Trails

What it means

Smooth ribbons of light left behind by moving cars, sparklers or dancers. It looks like the light is drawing lines through the air.

When to use it

Use for night streets, celebrations and movement. In still images it reads like a long-exposure photo; in video, expect streaks that follow motion.

Try this
"city bridge at night, red and white light trails"
Pairs well with

long exposure · neon glow · cityscape
