Liminal Found Footage

What it means

An old camcorder tape found in an empty, endless office lit by buzzing ceiling lights. The handheld camera breathes and sways, printed signs on the walls slowly go wrong, and the autofocus finds something that is gone a second later.

When to use it

Made for creepy shorts, horror teasers and spooky storytelling. It builds dread slowly, so it does not suit upbeat or quick messages.

Try this
"30-second horror teaser, liminal found footage, shaky camcorder walking through an empty yellow office, signs slowly changing"
