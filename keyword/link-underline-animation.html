Web Motion and Effects

Link Underline Animation

What it means

When you point at a link, a line draws itself underneath from left to right, then slides away when you move off.

When to use it

Use it for menus and text links so people can see what they are about to click. Works best with a few clear links, not long paragraphs full of them.
