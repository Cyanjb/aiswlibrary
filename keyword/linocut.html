Printing and Transfer Techniques

Linocut

What it means

A print carved into a block of lino, so you get bold chunky shapes, white gouge marks and solid flat ink.

When to use it

Use for punchy posters, folk-style animals and bold book illustrations. Not for fine soft detail.

Try this
"linocut print of a fox in the snow, black ink on cream paper"
Pairs well with

woodblock print · folk art · line drawing
