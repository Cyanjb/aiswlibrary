Loading Spinner

What it means

A spinning ring that tells you something is working in the background. Here it finishes by turning into a tick so you know the job is done.

When to use it

Use it for short waits of a few seconds, like saving or sending. For longer waits a progress bar or a skeleton screen tells people more.
