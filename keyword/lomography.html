Photo Types and Films

Lomography

What it means

Photos from cheap toy cameras with heavy dark corners, oversaturated colors and happy little mistakes. Bold, blurry, punchy and full of character.

When to use it

Use for playful, arty snapshots with loud color. If you want clean and sharp, this is the wrong word.

Try this
"lomography photo of a summer funfair, heavy vignette, saturated colors"
