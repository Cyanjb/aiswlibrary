Emotions and Moods

Longing

What it means

Wanting something just out of reach: a face pressed to a window, a hand on a closed door, someone watching a ship sail away.

When to use it

Use for romantic, hopeful or bittersweet scenes. Put distance or glass between the person and what they want.

Try this
"longing, woman on a pier watching a distant ship at dusk, wind in her shawl"
