Looking Over the Shoulder

What it means

Your subject turned away but glancing back toward the camera, as if they just heard their name. It feels candid, mysterious or flirty.

When to use it

Use for portraits and fashion shots that feel caught in a moment. Lovely with a trailing scarf or loose hair.

"woman looking over the shoulder at a rainy paris crosswalk, red umbrella"
