Architects

Louis Kahn

What it means

American architect (1901-1974) whose buildings feel like ancient monuments: massive concrete and brick walls, perfect circles and squares, and light pouring through huge cut-out openings.

When to use it

Use for serious, temple-like spaces full of shadow and sunbeams. Not the right pick for cozy or playful scenes.

Try this
"concrete courtyard facing the ocean, Louis Kahn style, late afternoon"
