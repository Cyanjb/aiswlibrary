Lovable Rogue

What it means

A charming scoundrel with a crooked grin, quick hands and a heart of gold underneath. Rules are more like suggestions.

When to use it

Use for adventure, heist and swashbuckling scenes. Show them mid-mischief, like picking a lock or dodging a guard, for more personality.

Try this
"lovable rogue grinning on a ship's rigging, golden hour"
