Camera Movements

Low Dolly-In

What it means

The camera moves slowly toward the subject from low down, about table height.

When to use it

Use it for product shots that should feel heroic and close. Say the height, or the model may drift back up to eye level.

Try this
"low dolly-in at table height toward a pink ceramic cup"
