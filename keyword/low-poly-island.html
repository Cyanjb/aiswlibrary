Low Poly Island

What it means

A tiny island made of flat faceted shapes floating in color, seen from a neat angle. Tiles rise from the sea with a splash, houses stack and trees pop up, each with a little note, and the light shows the time of day.

When to use it

Lovely for game-style intros, calm brand worlds and build-up explainers. The simple shapes keep it cute, so it is not for realistic products.
