Luis Barragan

What it means

Mexican architect (1902-1988) known for smooth walls painted hot pink, sunny yellow and deep blue, with still pools of water and sharp shadows. Simple shapes, bold color, total calm.

When to use it

Use for sunny, colorful courtyards and walls that photograph like paintings. Keep the scene uncluttered so the colors can sing.
