Lunarpunk

What it means

The nighttime sister of solarpunk, a peaceful, nature-loving future lit by moonlight, glowing plants and soft lanterns.

When to use it

Use for mystical, calm and magical night worlds. It is a newer word, so describe the glow and moonlight too.

Try this
"lunarpunk village, glowing mushrooms, moonlit river"
