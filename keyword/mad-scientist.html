Cliche Roles

Mad Scientist

What it means

A wild-haired genius in a lab coat, surrounded by bubbling flasks, sparks and strange machines. Brilliant, a bit unhinged, and always mid-experiment.

When to use it

Use for spooky, funny or sci-fi scenes full of fun props. It leans cartoonish, so add 'realistic' if you want it grounded.

Try this
"mad scientist in a stone lab, glowing green flasks, lightning outside"
