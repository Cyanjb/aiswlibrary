World Culture Styles

Madhubani Painting

What it means

A folk painting tradition from the Mithila region of Bihar, India, and nearby Nepal. Bold double outlines filled with bright color and pattern, showing fish, peacocks, gods and trees of life.

When to use it

Use for joyful, densely patterned scenes from nature or myth. Name it directly; a vague 'Indian art' gives a muddier result.
