Magical Girl

What it means

A beloved anime genre where everyday girls transform into sparkling heroines. Ribbons, wands, frills, starry light and twirling transformation poses.

When to use it

Use for bright, hopeful heroines and transformation moments. It leans sweet and sparkly, so it's not the pick for gritty fantasy.
