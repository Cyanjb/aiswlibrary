Magical Realism

What it means

Everyday life with one quiet impossible thing in it, treated as normal. A grandmother hangs laundry while yellow butterflies follow her, and nobody blinks.

When to use it

Use when you want gentle wonder inside a real, lived-in place. Keep the magic small and the rest ordinary, or it slides into full fantasy.
