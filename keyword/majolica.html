Pottery and Ceramics

Majolica

What it means

Glossy pottery with a milky white glaze painted in bright yellows, blues, greens and oranges, often with flowers, fruit and swirls.

When to use it

Use for cheerful painted plates, jugs and tiles with an Italian or Spanish feel. Busy patterns, so keep the setting simple.
