Transitions and Editing

Matched Zoom Transition

What it means

Shot A zooms fast into a detail; shot B starts tight on the same detail in a new place and pulls back.

When to use it

Use it for fast, smooth jumps between locations. Pick a detail both places can share, like an eye, a coin or a doorknob.

Try this
"shot A pushes in fast on a coffee cup rim in a cafe; shot B begins tight on the same rim at a mountain picnic and pulls back fast to reveal it"
