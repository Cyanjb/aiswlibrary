Maximalist Interior

What it means

More is more: layered patterns, bold wallpaper, stacks of books, plants, art covering every wall and bright clashing colors that somehow work.

When to use it

Use for joyful, personality-packed rooms full of things to look at. Can turn into visual noise, so name two or three main colors.

"maximalist living room, jungle wallpaper, velvet sofa, gallery wall"
