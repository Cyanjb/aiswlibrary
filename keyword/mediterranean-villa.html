Mediterranean Villa

What it means

Sun-washed white or warm plaster walls, red clay roof tiles, arched windows, terraces and bright flowers spilling over stone steps.

When to use it

Use for holiday, summer and seaside dream homes. Pairs naturally with blue sea and strong sun.
