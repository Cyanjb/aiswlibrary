Shot Sizes and Angles

Medium Close-Up

What it means

From the chest up: closer than a medium shot, looser than a close-up.

When to use it

Use for conversations that should feel personal without getting in someone's face.

Try this
"medium close-up of him leaning in to whisper"
Pairs well with

close-up · over-the-shoulder
