Lenses and Optics

Medium Depth of Field

What it means

Your subject is sharp and the background is soft, but you can still tell what is back there.

When to use it

Use it when the setting matters but should not steal attention. If you want a dreamy blur instead, ask for shallow depth of field.

Try this
"medium depth of field, a robot sharp in front, the city street behind it readable but soft"
