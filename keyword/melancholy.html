Melancholy

What it means

A soft, thoughtful sadness, like rain on a window or the last day of summer. Cool, faded colors, lowered eyes and lots of quiet space.

When to use it

Use when you want an image to feel tender and wistful rather than tragic. Pairs naturally with overcast light and a muted palette.

Try this
"melancholy, girl watching rain from a train window, muted colors"

overcast light · muted palette · fog
