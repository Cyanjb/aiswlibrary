Motion Graphics Styles

Memphis Animation

What it means

Loud 80s design in motion: squiggles, zigzags, confetti triangles and dotted circles in bright pastels with black outlines bounce into frame, cheerful and deliberately busy.

When to use it

Good for upbeat social reels, party promos and youthful brands. It is busy on purpose, so text needs a calm area or it gets lost.

Try this
"10-second promo, memphis animation, confetti triangles and squiggles bouncing in with black outlines on pastel pink"
