Microscope Story

What it means

One smooth journey down in scale, from a glass slide into the hidden world of cells, crystals or pollen, as it really looks under a microscope.

When to use it

Ideal for science, nature and health explainers. Keep the zoom continuous, because one long dive feels more magical than lots of cuts.
