Mid-Century Cutout

What it means

A 1960s cut-paper title sequence: flat, slightly wonky shapes in muted retro colors pop on and off to a swinging, jazzy rhythm, with hand-cut letters and lots of breathing room.

When to use it

Great for stylish title sequences, event promos and retro brand spots with a jazzy track. The playful swing feels off with modern tech or very serious content.
