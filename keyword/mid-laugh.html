Mid-Laugh

What it means

Caught in the middle of a real laugh, eyes squeezed, head tipped back, maybe a hand over the mouth. Natural joy that feels unposed.

When to use it

Use when stiff smiling portraits feel fake. Teeth and eyes can look a bit strange up close, so a slightly wider shot often helps.
