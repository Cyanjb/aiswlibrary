Mid-Leap

What it means

Your subject caught in the air at the top of a jump, feet off the ground, everything stretched out. It turns a still picture into a moment of motion.

When to use it

Use for dancers, athletes, kids and animals full of energy. Hands and feet can get odd in tricky poses, so try a few versions.
