Middle-Aged

What it means

Roughly between 40 and 60, with gentle signs of age like a few grey hairs and soft lines. A simple way to stop the AI from making everyone look 25.

When to use it

Use whenever age matters to the story. For more precision, give a decade, like 'in her fifties.'
