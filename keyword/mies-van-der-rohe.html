Architects

Ludwig Mies van der Rohe

What it means

German-American architect (1886-1969) famous for the motto 'less is more.' Glass walls, thin steel frames and open floors so simple they almost disappear.

When to use it

Use for quiet, elegant glass pavilions and towers. There is not much ornament here, so the light and setting do the work.
