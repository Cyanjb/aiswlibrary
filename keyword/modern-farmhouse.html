Modern Farmhouse

What it means

White walls, wooden beams, shiplap boards, a big farm sink and black metal lights. Country charm, kept clean and simple.

When to use it

Use for warm family kitchens and homey living rooms. It is very familiar, so add one surprise to keep it from feeling generic.
