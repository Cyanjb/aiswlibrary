Printing and Transfer Techniques

Monotype

What it means

A one-off print made by painting on a smooth plate and pressing paper onto it, giving soft, smudgy, painterly marks.

When to use it

Use for moody, loose, one-of-a-kind art prints. Expect a dreamy, imperfect finish, not crisp lines.
