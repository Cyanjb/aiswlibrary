Monstera

What it means

A big, glossy houseplant with heart-shaped leaves full of splits and holes. Tropical, bold and very photogenic.

When to use it

Use for jungle interiors, modern plant corners and leafy shadow patterns. Its leaf shape is easy for AI to get right.
