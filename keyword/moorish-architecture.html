Moorish Architecture

What it means

Palaces and courtyards from Islamic Spain and North Africa, with horseshoe arches, carved plaster lace, patterned tiles and quiet fountains.

When to use it

Use for rich, peaceful courtyards and palace halls full of pattern. Be respectful and specific about place when it matters to your story.
