Art Handicrafts

Mosaic

What it means

Pictures made from hundreds of tiny tiles, glass or broken pottery pieces set side by side, with thin lines of grout between them.

When to use it

Use for floors, walls, fountains and ancient or playful murals. Faces can get blocky, which is part of the charm.
