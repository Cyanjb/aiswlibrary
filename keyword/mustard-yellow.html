Basic Color Spectrum

Mustard Yellow

What it means

A warm, slightly brown yellow, softer than lemon and richer than butter. Very vintage, very cozy.

When to use it

Use for retro fashion, 70s rooms and warm autumn feels. It can look muddy next to gray, so pair it with blue, teal or burgundy.
