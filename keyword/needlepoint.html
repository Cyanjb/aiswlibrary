Needlepoint

What it means

Tiny even stitches of wool worked across a stiff canvas grid, filling every space to make a soft, pixel-like picture.

When to use it

Use for cushions, vintage samplers and cozy grandma-chic decor. The grid gives a slightly blocky look.
