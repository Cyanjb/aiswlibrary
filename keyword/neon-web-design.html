Web and App Design Styles

Neon Web Design

What it means

Electric glowing colors like neon signs, bright pinks, blues and greens on dark backgrounds, with bold contrast that pops off the screen.

When to use it

Use for nightlife, gaming, music and events. Glow effects get tiring and hard to read in big blocks of text.

Try this
"event page for a roller disco, neon web design, pink and blue glow on black"
