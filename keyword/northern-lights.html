Science and Elements

Northern Lights

What it means

Ribbons of green, and sometimes pink and purple, light rippling across a dark polar sky. Nature's own light show.

When to use it

Use for magical night landscapes and quiet wonder. Snowy ground and a still lake for reflections make it shine.

Try this
"northern lights over a snowy cabin, green ribbons reflected in a lake"

long exposure · first snow · cloudscape
