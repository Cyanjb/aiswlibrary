Notification Badge

What it means

A small bright circle with a number that sits on the corner of an icon, like a bell, to show how many new things are waiting. It pops in, counts up and clears once you look.

When to use it

Use it to point gently at new messages or updates. Too many badges at once start to feel like nagging, so save it for things that matter.

Try this
"Put a pink count badge on the bell icon in the header that pops in with a little bounce, makes the bell wiggle as the number goes up, and clears when I click the bell."
