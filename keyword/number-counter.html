Web Motion and Effects

Number Counter

What it means

Big numbers on stat cards count up quickly from zero to their real values and slow down as they land.

When to use it

Use it to make key numbers, like customers or ratings, catch the eye. Use real numbers only; a counting animation will not make a weak stat look stronger.

"Add three stat cards whose numbers count up from zero to 12,000+, 98% and 4.9 when they come into view, easing out at the end."
