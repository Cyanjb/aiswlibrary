Motion Graphics Styles

One Line Drawing Animation

What it means

One warm black ink line that never leaves the paper, drawn live while the camera follows the pen. At the end the camera pulls back to show every little scene was part of one big picture.

When to use it

Beautiful for brand stories, life journeys and thoughtful explainers. It is slow and minimal, so it is not built for lots of facts or text.
