Jewelry and Gemstones

Opal

What it means

A milky or dark gemstone that flashes little patches of rainbow color as it moves, like fire trapped inside. No two look the same.

When to use it

Use for magical, dreamy jewelry and fantasy treasure. Close-ups show the color play best; small stones in wide shots can turn plain white.

Try this
"opal ring flashing blue and green fire, macro shot on black velvet"
