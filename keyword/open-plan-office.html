Business

Open-Plan Office

What it means

A big, bright office with no walls between desks: long tables, laptops, plants, big windows and people working side by side.

When to use it

Use for company websites, hiring pages and work culture scenes. Ask for candid, real people to avoid stiff stock-photo poses.

Try this
"open-plan office with plants and big windows, team working at long desks, morning light"
