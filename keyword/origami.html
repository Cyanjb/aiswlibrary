Origami

What it means

Folded paper that comes to life: flat squares crease along sharp lines and fold into birds, boats or icons, each face catching a different shade from one soft light. Every reveal is a fold, never a fade.

When to use it

Nice for calm explainers, logo reveals and brands about ideas taking shape. Folds take time to read, so it is too slow for rapid-fire edits.

Try this
"8-second logo reveal, origami animation, a white paper square folding step by step into a crane"
