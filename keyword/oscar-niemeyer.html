Oscar Niemeyer

What it means

Brazilian architect (1907-2012) who bent white concrete into sweeping curves, domes and bowls. His buildings look like sculptures floating on reflecting pools.

When to use it

Use for bright, graceful modern buildings with a tropical, optimistic feel. Pairs beautifully with big skies and water.
