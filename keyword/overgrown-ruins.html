Overgrown Ruins

What it means

Old buildings swallowed by nature, with vines, trees and moss growing through broken walls and windows.

When to use it

Use for peaceful after-the-end worlds, lost temples and quiet adventure. Add light like golden hour or fog to set the feeling.

Try this
"overgrown ruins of a train station, vines, soft sunbeams"
