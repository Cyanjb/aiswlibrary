Fashion Patterns and Style

Oversized Streetwear

What it means

Baggy hoodies, roomy cargo pants, big puffer jackets and chunky sneakers, worn loose and layered. Relaxed, cool and very city.

When to use it

Use for urban portraits, skate parks and music videos. Avoid brand names; describe colors and pieces instead.

Try this
"teen in oversized streetwear, baggy hoodie and cargo pants, graffiti alley"
