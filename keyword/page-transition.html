Web Motion and Effects

Page Transition

What it means

When you click a link, a coloured panel sweeps across the screen and the next page appears behind it, instead of a sudden jump.

When to use it

Use it to make a site feel smooth and connected, like one continuous space. Keep it quick, around half a second, so it never slows people down.

Try this
"Add a page transition: when I click a link, a pink panel wipes across the screen and reveals the new page, and it wipes back the other way when I go back."
