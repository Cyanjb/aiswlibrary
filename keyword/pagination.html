UI Components

Pagination

What it means

A row of numbered page buttons with previous and next arrows under a list. The current page is highlighted, and clicking a number jumps to that page of results.

When to use it

Use it when a list is too long for one screen and people like to know where they are, like search results or a shop. For casual browsing, endless scrolling can feel smoother.
