Paisley

What it means

Curving teardrop shapes packed with tiny swirls and flowers. The motif came from Persia and India, and was named after the Scottish town that wove it into shawls.

When to use it

Use for rich textiles, bandanas, wallpaper and boho fashion. It gets busy fast, so give it room or limit the colors.
