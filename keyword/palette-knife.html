Palette Knife

What it means

Paint spread with a flat metal knife instead of a brush, leaving smooth, chunky slabs of color with sharp edges. Bold and almost sculpted.

When to use it

Use for bright landscapes, city lights and flowers with lots of energy. Fine detail gets lost, so think in big shapes.
