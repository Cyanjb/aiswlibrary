Camera Movements

Pan Onto Subject

What it means

The camera turns from somewhere else and comes to rest on your subject.

When to use it

Use it for a small reveal, or to guide the eye to your hero. Keep it slow, because a fast version starts to look like a whip pan.

Try this
"the camera pans across a kitchen shelf and settles on a pink cup"
