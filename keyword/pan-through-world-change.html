Transitions and Editing

Pan-Through World Change

What it means

One continuous sideways pan where the vehicle and the world change each time the subject passes behind a pillar.

When to use it

Use it for playful journeys or product adventures. Give each world a clear, different look so every change reads.

Try this
"one continuous side-on pan following a cyclist; behind each pillar the bike and the world change, from city to desert to snowy village"

foreground wipe · truck shot · background swap
