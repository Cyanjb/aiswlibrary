Camera Movements

Pan

What it means

The camera stays in one spot and turns left or right, like turning your head.

When to use it

Use to follow someone across a room, or to reveal what is beside them.

Try this
"slow pan right across the busy market"
Pairs well with

tilt-up · tracking-shot

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
