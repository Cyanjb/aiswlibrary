Paper Cut Lightbox

What it means

A layered paper shadow box lit from behind: sheets of cut card stacked in a shallow box, warm light glowing through the paper, windows and moons shining through holes. Layers slide and pivot like paper on sticks, and the lamp turning on is the big moment.

When to use it

Gorgeous for bedtime stories, holiday cards and cozy brand moments. Paper only slides and turns, so it cannot do bendy, stretchy action.
