Motion Graphics Styles

Paper Map Journey

What it means

A journey told on a paper map: a dashed route draws itself from place to place, pins drop with a little bounce, place names stamp on, and the camera glides and tilts to follow the line.

When to use it

Great for travel stories, company histories, delivery routes and event recaps. It needs a real route with a few clear stops, or there is nothing to follow.

"20-second travel story, paper map journey animation, dashed route drawing across an old map, pins bouncing in at each city"
