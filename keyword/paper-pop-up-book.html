Paper Pop-up Book

What it means

A pop-up book on a real desk becomes a stage: paper scenery springs up from the page, flat cut-out characters perform, and turning the page changes the scene.

When to use it

Charming for kids' stories, invitations, travel stories and gentle explainers. Every change should come from a fold, a pull-tab or a page turn.

"20-second bedtime story, paper pop-up book, a castle unfolding from the page, a cut-out dragon sliding in on a pull-tab"
