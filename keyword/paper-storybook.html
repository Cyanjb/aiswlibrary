Paper Storybook

What it means

A warm handmade picture book where every character and prop is a paper sticker with a white border, layered on textured paper. Stickers peel on with a little bounce, timed to the narrator's words, and everything gently wiggles like stop motion.

When to use it

Lovely for gentle story explainers, kids' content and cozy brand stories with a voiceover. It is slow and sweet, so it will not suit fast, hard-selling ads.
