Paper Texture Web Design

What it means

A site that feels like real printed paper, with soft paper grain, very few colors and a clean mix of Montserrat and Roboto type.

When to use it

Use for stationery shops, writers, studios and handmade brands. The texture can look dull if the content is thin.

Try this
"website for a stationery shop, paper texture web design, cream paper grain, black ink"
