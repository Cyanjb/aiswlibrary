Web Motion and Effects

Parallax Scrolling

What it means

Layers of a page move at different speeds as you scroll, so the background drifts slowly while the front races past. It makes a flat page feel deep, like looking out of a train window.

When to use it

Use it on a hero, the big opening area of a page, to give a landing page a sense of depth. Keep it gentle: strong movement can make some people feel queasy.

Try this
"Add a parallax hero with a sky, far hills and near hills that each move at a different speed as I scroll"
