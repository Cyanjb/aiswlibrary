Web and App Design Styles

Parchment Editorial UI

What it means

Pages that look printed on warm ivory parchment, with near-black ink, calm editorial type and just one earthy clay accent used sparingly. It feels like a thoughtful research journal.

When to use it

Use for writing tools, research sites, essays and thoughtful knowledge products. Too quiet for flashy sales pages or kids' apps.
