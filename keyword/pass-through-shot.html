Camera Movements

Pass-Through Shot

What it means

The camera flies straight through a gap, like a window, a fence, a crowd or a ring, and carries on in the same scene.

When to use it

Use to make a space feel continuous and alive. Name the opening clearly. If you want to come out in a new scene, use Push-Through instead.

Try this
"the camera flies through the open kitchen window and glides across the busy room to the family at the table"
