Jewelry and Gemstones

Pearls

What it means

Smooth, round gems grown inside oysters and mussels, with a soft, glowing shine. They come in white, cream, pink, gold and dark grey, and in lumpy shapes called baroque pearls.

When to use it

Use for classic elegance on necklaces, earrings and hair pieces. Add "baroque pearls" for irregular shapes with a more artistic feel.
