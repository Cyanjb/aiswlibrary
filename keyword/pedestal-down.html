Camera Movements

Pedestal Down

What it means

The whole camera lowers straight down without tilting.

When to use it

Use it to land on a subject or drop to its eye level. Some models tilt instead of lowering, so say straight down.

Try this
"the camera lowers straight down to the height of a pink cup on a table"
Pairs well with

pedestal · tilt up · eye level
