Camera Movements

Pedestal

What it means

The whole camera rises or lowers straight up and down, without tilting.

When to use it

Use to rise above a scene and show its layout, or to drop down to eye level.

Try this
"the camera pedestals up from the table into a top-down view"
Pairs well with

crane-shot · tilt-up

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
