Pencil Sketch Web Design

What it means

A friendly hand-drawn site that looks like pencil drawings on warm cream paper, with soft teal accents, handwritten headings, dashed card outlines and chunky pencil-style shadows.

When to use it

Use for creative classes, kids' learning, journaling apps and small makers. Can feel unfinished for serious finance or health brands.
