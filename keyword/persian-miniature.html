Persian Miniature

What it means

Small, richly detailed paintings made for illustrated books in Persia, today's Iran. Flat, layered spaces, jewel colors, patterned carpets and gardens, often edged in gold.

When to use it

Use for storytelling scenes full of tiny figures, palaces and gardens. It doesn't do deep, realistic perspective, and that's part of its beauty.
