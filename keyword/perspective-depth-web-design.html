Perspective Depth Web Design

What it means

A site that breaks out of flatness with isometric views (3D seen from a tilted angle), vanishing points and stacked layers that pull your eye through the page.

When to use it

Use for tech products, games, architecture and product tours. Can be heavy to build and slow on older phones.
