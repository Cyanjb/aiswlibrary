Body Types

Petite

What it means

A shorter, small-framed body. Clothes and furniture look a little bigger around them, and they read clearly as an adult, not a child.

When to use it

Use to show shorter adults in fashion, office and everyday scenes. Add an age or role, like "petite woman in her forties", so the AI keeps them grown up.

Try this
"petite woman in her forties in a tailored blazer, city crosswalk"
Pairs well with

tailored suit · candid · wide shot
