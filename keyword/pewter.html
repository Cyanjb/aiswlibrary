Metals

Pewter

What it means

A soft, dull grey metal with a gentle, low shine, often seen in old mugs, plates and candlesticks. Quiet and old-world.

When to use it

Use for medieval feasts, rustic tables and muted still lifes. It is less flashy than silver, which is the point.
