Pieter Bruegel the Elder

What it means

(c. 1525-1569) Flemish painter of busy village life seen from above: peasant weddings, frozen ponds full of skaters and snowy hunters coming home. Earthy colors and many tiny people.

When to use it

Use for lively crowd scenes and wintry old village landscapes. Faces are small and simple, so it is not for portraits.
