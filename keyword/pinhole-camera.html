Pinhole Camera

What it means

A tiny hole instead of a lens, giving soft, dreamy pictures with dark edges and everything gently in focus.

When to use it

Use for quiet, timeless and slightly ghostly images. Nothing is sharp, so it is not the choice for crisp detail.

Try this
"pinhole camera photo of an old pier at dawn, soft vignette"
