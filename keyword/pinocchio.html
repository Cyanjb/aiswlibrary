Pinocchio

What it means

The wooden puppet from the original 1883 Italian story, carved by an old woodcarver, with a nose that grows when he lies. In the book he is a cheeky troublemaker in a paper suit and bark shoes. Describe the book version, not later cartoon versions.

When to use it

Use for storybook mischief, workshops full of wood shavings and fairy tale lessons. Ask for 'carved wood texture' to keep him a real puppet.
