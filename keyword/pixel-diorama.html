Pixel Diorama

What it means

Flat pixel-art characters standing like paper cutouts inside a lit, foggy 3D miniature set, seen from above with a dreamy blur at the edges. Lamps cast real shadows and the light is the star.

When to use it

Lovely for game-style trailers, cozy fantasy stories and atmospheric intros. Building the set takes time, so it suits a few rich scenes rather than many quick ones.

Try this
"15-second fantasy intro, pixel diorama animation, pixel characters in a foggy 3d miniature town, lanterns glowing, camera gliding above"
