Plucky Sidekick

What it means

The small, brave, loyal friend who follows the hero everywhere, ready with a joke or a rescue. Full of heart and a little bit clumsy.

When to use it

Use for adventure and comedy scenes that need energy and warmth. Show them beside the hero so the role reads clearly.
