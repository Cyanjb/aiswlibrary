Body Types

Plus-Size

What it means

A fuller, larger body shown naturally and confidently. AI tends to slim everyone down by default, so this word helps it show the many people who wear larger sizes.

When to use it

Use whenever you want real, everyday representation in fashion, lifestyle and portrait scenes. Pair it with clothing and an activity so the person is the story, not their size.

Try this
"plus-size woman laughing with friends at a picnic, linen dress, sunny park"
