Storytelling

Point of No Return

What it means

The moment a character commits and can't go back: stepping off the cliff, closing the door behind them, crossing the bridge as it burns.

When to use it

Use for brave, decisive moments in a story. Show what is being left behind as clearly as where they are going.

"point of no return, knight stepping onto a rope bridge as it frays, back to camera"
