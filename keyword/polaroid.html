Polaroid

What it means

An instant photo with a thick white border and soft, slightly faded colors. Creamy highlights, gentle blur and a real snapshot feeling.

When to use it

Use for nostalgic, personal, party or road trip images. Say Polaroid photo with white frame if you want the border to show.
