Power Suit

What it means

A bold suit made to command a room: strong padded shoulders, sharp lapels and a confident color like red, white or black. Very 1980s boardroom, still powerful today.

When to use it

Use for leaders, bosses and confident portraits of any gender. Push it with a low angle for extra authority.
