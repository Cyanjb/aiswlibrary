Web Motion and Effects

Preloader

What it means

A full screen loading page that counts up to 100 percent, then slides away to reveal the website underneath.

When to use it

Use it on portfolio or launch sites where a short, stylish entrance fits the mood. Keep it brief; making people wait for a pretty counter on every visit can annoy them.

Try this
"Add a preloader to my site: a dark screen with a big percentage counting to 100 and a progress bar, then it slides up to show the page."
Pairs well with

staggered reveal · page transition · bold web design
