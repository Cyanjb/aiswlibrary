Pressed Flowers and Ink

What it means

Real flattened flowers and leaves combined with ink drawing, so petals become a dress, hair or wings on a sketched figure.

When to use it

Use for gentle, nature-filled art and botanical portraits. It works best on plain paper backgrounds, which keep it clean.
