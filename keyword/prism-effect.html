Photography Special Effects

Prism Effect

What it means

Holding a glass prism in front of the lens to throw rainbows, ghostly double edges and soft color streaks across the picture.

When to use it

Use for dreamy portraits and music photos with a touch of magic. Too much can hide the face, so ask for rainbows at the edge of the frame.

Try this
"portrait with prism effect, rainbow light streaks along the edge of the frame"
