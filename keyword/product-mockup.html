Product Mockup

What it means

A clean, realistic picture showing a product, package or screen design in a real setting, like a box on a table or an app on a phone in someone's hand.

When to use it

Use to preview packaging, labels and app screens before they exist. AI often garbles small text, so add real lettering afterward in a design app.

Try this
"product mockup of a plain kraft paper coffee bag on a marble counter, soft daylight"
Pairs well with

soft key · shallow depth of field · macro photography
