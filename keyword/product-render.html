Product Render

What it means

A spotless studio shot of an object made in 3D, floating or sitting on a simple backdrop with clean, flattering light.

When to use it

Use for packaging ideas, gadgets and mockups. Real logos and tiny printed text often come out garbled, so keep labels simple.

"wireless earbuds on a pastel pedestal, product render"
