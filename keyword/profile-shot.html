Shot Sizes and Angles

Profile Shot

What it means

The subject seen exactly side-on.

When to use it

Use for silhouettes, faces deep in thought, or two people facing off.

Try this
"profile shot of him staring out of the train window"
Pairs well with

close-up · rim-lighting

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
