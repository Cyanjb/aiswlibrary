Projection on White Model

What it means

A pure white scale model of a real place sits in a dark room, and a projector paints maps, colors and data onto it one layer at a time.

When to use it

Great for architecture, city planning, property and climate explainers. It shows information sitting on real shapes, so keep the model plain white.
