Prussian Blue

What it means

A deep, inky blue with a touch of green, made in Berlin around 1706. It is the blue of old blueprints and famous Japanese wave prints.

When to use it

Use for rich night skies, ink art and moody seas. It can feel almost black in small images, so give it some light.
