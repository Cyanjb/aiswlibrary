Style Suffixes

-punk

What it means

From cyberpunk and steampunk, -punk means 'imagine a whole world powered by this.' Solarpunk runs on sunlight and gardens; steampunk runs on brass and steam.

When to use it

Use to invent a world with its own technology and attitude. Pair it with a material or era so the AI knows what the world is built from.

Try this
"solarpunk village with rooftop gardens, bright morning"

biopunk rococo · cybernetic punk · brushed metal
