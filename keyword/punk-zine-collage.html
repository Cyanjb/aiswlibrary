Punk Zine Collage

What it means

A homemade photocopied zine that builds itself: black and white cutouts, ransom-note letters and tape slap onto grainy paper, all a little crooked, with one loud accent color. Pieces jump in with choppy, hand-placed steps instead of smooth slides.

When to use it

Great for bold, cheeky reels, ads and hot takes that want attitude. Too noisy for calm or serious topics, and it falls apart if you use more than one accent color.
