Purple Dashboard UI

What it means

A clean app control center with a purple theme, navigation along the top bar, tidy cards and quick, developer-friendly screens.

When to use it

Use for dashboards, hosting tools and team apps where people check on projects and push out updates. Not meant for marketing or storytelling pages.
