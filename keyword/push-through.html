Transitions and Editing

Push-Through

What it means

The camera pushes into something dark and round, like a peephole or a hole, and comes out in the next scene.

When to use it

Use for curiosity, spy moods and down-the-rabbit-hole jumps.

Try this
"the camera pushes into the keyhole and comes out in the next room"
