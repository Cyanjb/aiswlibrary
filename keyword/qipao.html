Worldwide Fashion

Qipao

What it means

A fitted Chinese dress with a high mandarin collar, side slits and frog-knot buttons, often in silk with embroidered flowers. Also called a cheongsam.

When to use it

Use for elegant portraits and 1930s Shanghai scenes. It is worn for weddings and Lunar New Year, so treat it as special dress, not a costume.
