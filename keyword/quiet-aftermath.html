Quiet Aftermath

What it means

The still moment after something big has happened. Confetti on an empty floor, smoke drifting over a field, a tired hero sitting in the dust.

When to use it

Use when you want feeling without action, letting the viewer imagine what came before. Soft light and empty space make it stronger.

Try this
"quiet aftermath, empty ballroom at dawn, fallen streamers, one guest asleep"
