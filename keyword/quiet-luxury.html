Quiet Luxury

What it means

Simple, expensive-looking clothes with no logos: cashmere knits, camel coats, soft trousers and perfect fit in cream, beige and grey. Rich without shouting about it.

When to use it

Use for calm, upscale lifestyle and fashion images. It can look plain in a busy scene, so give it a clean, airy setting.
