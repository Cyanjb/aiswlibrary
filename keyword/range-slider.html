Range Slider

What it means

A bar with a round knob you drag left or right to pick a number. The bar fills in colour as you go and a little bubble shows the value.

When to use it

Use it for settings with lots of in-between values, like brightness, volume or price. For exact numbers people may still want a box they can type into.

Try this
"Add a range slider for brightness from 0 to 100, with a pink filled track and a value bubble that follows the knob while I drag."
