Ray Tracing

What it means

A 3D look where light bounces like it does in real life, giving true reflections in puddles, glass and shiny floors.

When to use it

Use when reflections matter, like rainy streets, mirrors or chrome. It is a hint toward realism, not a magic fix for a weak prompt.

Try this
"rainy neon alley at night, ray tracing, reflections in puddles"
