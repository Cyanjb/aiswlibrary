Reaching Out

What it means

An arm stretched toward something or someone: a hand reaching for a falling star, a friend, a door, a light. It makes the viewer feel the wanting.

When to use it

Use for emotional moments of hope, rescue or longing. Show what they are reaching for, or the pose can feel unfinished.
