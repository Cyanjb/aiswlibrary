Red Fox

What it means

A slim fox with flame-orange fur, a white chest, black socks and a bushy tail. Clever, alert and gorgeous against snow or autumn leaves.

When to use it

Use for storybook woodland scenes and wildlife portraits. It pops most against cool blue or green backgrounds.
