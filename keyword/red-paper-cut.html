Motion Graphics Styles

Red Paper Cut

What it means

Chinese red paper window cuts brought to life: jointed red cutouts laid on paper, where the cut is the drawing and the holes let the light through. Characters move in crisp steps, and one folded cut opens into a full rosette.

When to use it

Lovely for festive greetings, new year posts and folk stories. It is very strongly linked to its tradition, so use it with respect and the right occasion.
