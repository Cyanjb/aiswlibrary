Reflection Shot

What it means

The scene shown in a mirror, puddle, lake or shop window, sometimes upside down or doubled. Instant symmetry and a touch of mystery.

When to use it

Use for clever compositions and calm, mirrored scenes. Say where the reflection is, like reflected in a puddle, for the best results.

Try this
"city lights reflected in a rain puddle, close to the ground, night"
