Fashion Timeline and History

Regency Era Fashion

What it means

Early 1800s dress with a waistline just under the bust, light white muslin falling straight to the floor, short puffed sleeves, bonnets and little cropped jackets.

When to use it

Use for period romance, country house scenes and garden walks. Say "empire waist" too if the waistline keeps slipping down.

Try this
"young woman in a white regency era muslin gown, walking through an english garden"
