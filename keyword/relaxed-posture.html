Relaxed Posture

What it means

Loose shoulders, an easy stance and a body at rest, like someone leaning on a wall or settled into a chair. The opposite of stiff and posed.

When to use it

Use to make people look comfortable and natural instead of mannequin-like. Add the action, like leaning or sitting, for the best results.
