Rembrandt

What it means

(1606-1669) Dutch master of portraits glowing out of deep darkness. Warm golden light, brown shadows and faces full of feeling.

When to use it

Use for moody, warm portraits lit by one strong light. His name is also a portrait lighting style in photography.
