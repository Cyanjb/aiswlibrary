Web Motion and Effects

Ripple Click

What it means

When you click a button, a soft circle spreads out from the exact spot you touched and fades away inside the button.

When to use it

Use it to make buttons feel responsive and alive. Keep it subtle; a loud ripple on every small button gets tiring.

Try this
"Add a click ripple to my buttons: a soft white circle grows from where I click and fades out, staying inside the button."
