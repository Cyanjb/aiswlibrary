Printing and Transfer Techniques

Risograph

What it means

A grainy stencil printing look with bright see-through inks like fluoro pink and teal that overlap into new colors.

When to use it

Use for zines, indie posters and playful illustrations with a soft, grainy texture. Fine detail can blur.

Try this
"risograph print of a cat in a plant shop, pink and teal ink"

screen print · doodle · pastel palette
