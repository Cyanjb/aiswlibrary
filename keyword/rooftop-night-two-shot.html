Camera Movements

Rooftop Night Two-Shot

What it means

Two people on a rooftop at night, the camera drifting between each face and a shot of both together.

When to use it

Use it for quiet night scenes between two characters. City lights behind them do a lot of the work, so mention them.

Try this
"two friends on a rooftop at night, city lights behind, the camera drifting between each face and the two-shot"
