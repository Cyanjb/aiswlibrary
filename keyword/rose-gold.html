Rose Gold

What it means

Gold mixed with copper to give a warm, pinkish blush color. Softer and more romantic than yellow gold.

When to use it

Use for jewelry, watches and product shots with a warm, modern, gentle feel. Pairs nicely with pastel backgrounds.
