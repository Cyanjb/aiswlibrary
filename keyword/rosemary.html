Rosemary

What it means

A woody herb with needle-like, silvery green leaves and tiny pale blue flowers. Rustic and fresh, like a cottage kitchen.

When to use it

Use for herb gardens, rustic kitchen still lifes and cooking scenes. Small sprigs make a nice garnish detail in food shots.
