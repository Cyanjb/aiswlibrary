Illustration and Animation

Rotoscope

What it means

Animation traced over real filmed movement, so drawn characters move with lifelike weight and grace. It sits halfway between live action and drawing.

When to use it

Use when you want drawn characters that move naturally, like a dancer or a runner. In video, expect a slightly wobbly, dreamy line.
