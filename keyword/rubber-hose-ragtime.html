Rubber Hose Ragtime

What it means

A scratchy old-time ink cartoon where characters, props and even buildings bounce to a jaunty band. Arms and legs swing like garden hoses with no elbows, and scenes close on a shrinking circle.

When to use it

Great for playful intros, music-driven ads and retro-themed shorts. Everything bounces all the time, so it can feel too goofy for serious messages.
