Running Toward Camera

What it means

Your subject racing straight at the viewer, hair and clothes flying, getting bigger in the frame. It pulls you right into the action.

When to use it

Use for chases, joyful reunions and big action. It is strongest in video; in a still image, add motion blur so it doesn't look frozen.

Try this
"kids running toward camera through a sprinkler, summer afternoon, slow motion"

slow motion · tracking shot · low angle
