Camera Movements

Rush-By Shot

What it means

A low camera with a wide lens races right past the subject.

When to use it

Use it for pure speed. The wide lens makes the rush feel even faster, but fine detail can blur.

Try this
"a wide-lens camera at knee height hurtles past a skateboarder, who swings across the frame, horizon rolling"
