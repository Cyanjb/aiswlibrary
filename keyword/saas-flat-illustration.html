SaaS Flat Illustration

What it means

Friendly, simple vector drawings used on software websites: flat colors, rounded people with no fine detail, floating charts, cloud icons and laptops.

When to use it

Use for websites, onboarding screens and explainer graphics. It can feel generic, so pick a brand-like color palette to make it yours.
