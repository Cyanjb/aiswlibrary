Sandro Botticelli

What it means

(c. 1445-1510) Italian Renaissance painter of graceful goddesses with flowing hair, floating fabrics and flower-strewn gardens. Pale, light colors and elegant lines.

When to use it

Use for romantic, mythical, springtime figures. It shines with flowers and flowing hair, less so with modern scenes.
