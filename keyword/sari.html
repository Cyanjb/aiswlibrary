Worldwide Fashion

Sari

What it means

A long, unstitched length of cloth, usually five to nine yards, draped around the body over a fitted blouse, with the decorated end falling over the shoulder. Worn across South Asia in many regional styles.

When to use it

Use for everyday portraits, festivals and weddings. Richly woven silk saris, often red, are bridal wear in many regions, so name the occasion and color you want.
