Satellite Briefing

What it means

Real satellite views of a place, turned grey like an analyst's screen, with thin white lines, target boxes and measurements drawn on the ground.

When to use it

Made for news, investigations, geography and serious explainers. It reads as factual, so use it for real places and true stories.
