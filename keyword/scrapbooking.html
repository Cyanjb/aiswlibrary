Scrapbooking

What it means

Pages layered with photos, washi tape, ticket stubs, pressed flowers and handwritten notes. A happy, collected mess of memories.

When to use it

Use for nostalgic flat lays, journaling vibes, or a memory-board look. Any written words may come out garbled, so keep the text small or skip it.
