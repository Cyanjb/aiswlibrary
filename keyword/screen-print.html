Printing and Transfer Techniques

Screen Print

What it means

Flat, bold layers of ink pushed through a mesh screen. Bright solid colors, slightly off-register edges, like gig posters and pop art.

When to use it

Use for poppy posters, t-shirt graphics and bold portraits. Limit to a few colors for the most believable look.

"screen print poster of a surfer, three colors, slightly misaligned"
