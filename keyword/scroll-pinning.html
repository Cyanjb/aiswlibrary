Web Motion and Effects

Scroll Pinning

What it means

A section freezes in place while you keep scrolling, and its inside changes step by step, like a slideshow you drive with the scroll wheel. When the steps are done, the page moves on.

When to use it

Use it to walk people through a process or a product story in clear steps. It can feel like the page is stuck if the steps take too long, so keep it to three or four.

Try this
"Pin the How it works section while I scroll and swap through steps 01, 02 and 03, then let the page continue"
