Web Motion and Effects

Scroll Progress Bar

What it means

A thin coloured bar across the top of the page grows from empty to full as you read down. It shows at a glance how much of the article is left.

When to use it

Use it on blog posts, guides and long articles so readers know how far they have come. It adds little on short pages.
