Web Motion and Effects

Scroll Snap

What it means

The page is made of full-screen panels, and each scroll glides to the next one and settles exactly on it. You never stop halfway between two panels.

When to use it

Use it for story pages, product tours or galleries where each screen is one idea. It can feel bossy on pages with lots of reading, so save it for short, visual sections.

Try this
"Make my page a stack of full-height panels that snap into place one at a time as I scroll"
