Segmented Control

What it means

A pill split into a few side by side choices, with a coloured highlight that slides to the one you pick. Only one choice is on at a time.

When to use it

Use it to switch between two to four views of the same thing, like Day, Week and Month. With more options, use a dropdown menu instead.
