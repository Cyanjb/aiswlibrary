Seigaiha

What it means

Overlapping half circles stacked like fish scales, each one a little wave. A traditional Japanese pattern meaning calm seas and good luck.

When to use it

Use for kimonos, wrapping paper, tiles and calm ocean themes. Ask for gold or indigo lines to keep it looking refined.
