Techniques

Sfumato

What it means

Soft, smoky blending where edges melt into shadow with no hard lines. A Renaissance painting method that gives faces a hushed, mysterious glow.

When to use it

Use for gentle, timeless portraits and quiet moods. It softens everything, so it's not the pick for crisp detail.

Try this
"portrait of a young scholar, sfumato, warm dim light"
