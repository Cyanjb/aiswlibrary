Shadow Art

What it means

A pile of random objects that casts a shadow of something totally different, like a heap of junk making the shadow of a dancer.

When to use it

Use for surprising reveals and clever art pieces. The AI may struggle to match the shadow, so keep the shadow shape simple.

Try this
"pile of old shoes casting a shadow of a cat on the wall"
Pairs well with

hard light · silhouette · found object art
