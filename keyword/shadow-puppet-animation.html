Shadow Puppet Animation

What it means

A shadow theatre in a warm lamp-lit room: black jointed puppets move across a glowing paper screen, swinging on their joints, blurring as they step away from the screen and flickering softly in the light.

When to use it

Lovely for folk tales, bedtime stories and moody storytelling. Faces and small details do not show in silhouette, so it suits simple shapes and clear actions.
