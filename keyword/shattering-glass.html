Shattering Glass

What it means

Glass breaking into a burst of sharp, glittering shards, either frozen in midair or flying outward. Instant drama.

When to use it

Use for action, product shots and big surprise moments. Hands and faces close to the shards can come out messy, so give them space.

Try this
"perfume bottle shattering, glass shards frozen midair, black background"

slow motion · freeze frame · hard light
