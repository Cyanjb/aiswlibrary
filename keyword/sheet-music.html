Sheet Music

What it means

Pages of musical notes on lined staffs, often aged and curling at the edges. Romantic and a little old-world.

When to use it

Use for vintage still lifes, romantic scenes and paper-texture backgrounds. The notes will not be real music, so do not count on readable melodies.
