Sherlock Holmes

What it means

The sharp-eyed detective from the original stories, tall and thin with a hawk-like nose, as first drawn in old magazine illustrations with a deerstalker cap and travelling cape. Ask for the book version, not later screen versions.

When to use it

Use for Victorian mysteries, foggy London streets and clever detective portraits. Mention 'Victorian illustration' to keep him true to the books.
