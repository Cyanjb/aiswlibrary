Anime and Manga

Shonen Style

What it means

The bold, high-energy look of action manga made mainly for boys and teens. Spiky hair, determined faces, sharp angles and big dynamic poses.

When to use it

Use for battles, sports and hero moments full of movement. It can feel loud, so it's less suited to quiet scenes.
