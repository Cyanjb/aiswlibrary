Shoujo Style

What it means

The romantic look of manga made mainly for girls and young women. Huge sparkling eyes, flowing hair, and flowers or sparkles floating behind the characters.

When to use it

Use for tender, emotional and romantic moments. Add 'retro' if you want the older, softer look with lots of dotted shading.
