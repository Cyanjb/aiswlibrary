UI Components

Side Drawer

What it means

A menu panel that slides in from the edge of the screen when you click the three line menu icon. The page behind it dims until you close it.

When to use it

Use it to tuck away navigation, the list of links to other pages, so the main screen stays clean. People may miss links hidden in it, so keep key actions visible.

Try this
"Make a side drawer menu that slides in from the left when I click the menu icon, dims the page and closes when I click outside it"
