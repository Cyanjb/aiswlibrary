Silent Film Animation

What it means

An old silent comedy as it would look projected: an engraved ink drawing with silver wash, on flickering, wobbling, scratched film in a square frame between velvet curtains, told in wide shots and title cards to piano music. Figures move in a slightly jerky hand-cranked way.

When to use it

Charming for vintage-themed stories, comedy shorts and nostalgic brand moments. There is no voice at all, so the story has to read through action and title cards.

Try this
"20-second comedy short, silent film animation, flickering scratched black and white film, a figure chasing a hat, title card between shots"
