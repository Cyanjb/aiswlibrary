TV and Movie Culture

Silent Film

What it means

Old black and white movies from before sound: flickering grain, jerky fast motion, big expressive acting and title cards with fancy borders.

When to use it

Use for charm, comedy and early 1900s nostalgia. Expect the faces to be exaggerated, because the actors had to tell the story without words.
