Silkscreen Poster Animation

What it means

A vintage travel poster printed on camera: one flat ink per pull of the squeegee, stepped color bands instead of smooth blends, thin gaps where layers do not line up, and the key facts printed last. There are no fades at all.

When to use it

Great for event posters, travel promos and music announcements. Fine details and smooth blends do not suit it, so keep the art bold and flat.

"12-second event promo, silkscreen poster animation, flat ink layers printing a mountain poster one pull at a time, date printing last"
