Food and Cooking Effects

Sizzle

What it means

Food hitting a hot pan or grill with popping oil, little bursts of smoke and browning edges. You can almost hear it.

When to use it

Use for cooking action, grills and street food scenes. It works best in video, where the bubbling oil can move.

Try this
"steak sizzling in a cast iron pan, oil spatter, smoky kitchen light"
