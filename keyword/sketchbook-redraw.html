Sketchbook Redraw

What it means

A cream spiral sketchbook where each idea is drawn three times: the caption writes in, a pencil sketch wipes across, then the colored version wipes over it, with a pencil riding along each wipe.

When to use it

Great for concept explainers, definitions and kid-friendly lessons. The three-step wipe is busy for a moment at every change, so give each idea a few seconds.
