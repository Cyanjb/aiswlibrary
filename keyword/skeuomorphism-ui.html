Web and App Design Styles

Skeuomorphism UI

What it means

Screens that copy real objects, like leather notebooks, wooden shelves and metal knobs, with textures and 3D shading so everything feels touchable.

When to use it

Use for music apps, games, calculators and nostalgic tools. Can look old-fashioned and heavy next to modern flat sites.
