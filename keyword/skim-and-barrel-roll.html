Skim and Barrel Roll

What it means

The camera skims just over the subject while rolling a full circle.

When to use it

Use it for a wild drone move nobody would risk this close in real life. It is a lot, so save it for one moment.

Try this
"the camera skims just over a red sports car while barrel-rolling a full 360 degrees, then falls away behind"
