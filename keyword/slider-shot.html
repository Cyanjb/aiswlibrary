Camera Movements

Slider Shot

What it means

A short, smooth slide sideways, like the camera is sitting on a little rail.

When to use it

Use it for polished product footage, where things up front glide past faster than things behind. Put something in the foreground, or the slide is hard to notice.

Try this
"smooth slider move past a pink cup, a blurred vase gliding by in the foreground"
Pairs well with

truck shot · shallow depth of field · low dolly-in

Works in
