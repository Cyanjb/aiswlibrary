Slot Machine Hook

What it means

A pastel slot machine spins through options, slows with a satisfying click-click, lands on today's pick with a ding and a gold burst, then two comic panels explain it with zooms and pans.

When to use it

Perfect as a series opener like tool of the day or pick of the week. It is a hook, not a whole video, so it needs a short explainer after it.

Try this
"15-second series opener, slot machine hook animation, pastel reels spinning and landing on today's pick with a gold burst"
