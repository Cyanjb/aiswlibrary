Smoke Bomb

What it means

A burst of thick colored smoke filling the air around a subject. Pink, orange or teal clouds that swirl and glow in the light.

When to use it

Use for bold fashion, wedding and music shots with big color. Pick one or two smoke colors for the cleanest look.
