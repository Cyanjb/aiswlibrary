Snow Leopard

What it means

A big wild cat with smoky gray fur, dark rosettes, pale eyes and a huge fluffy tail. It looks like winter itself turned into an animal.

When to use it

Use for icy mountain scenes and quiet, powerful portraits. Say snow leopard in full, or you may get a regular leopard standing in snow.
