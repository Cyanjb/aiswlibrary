Soft Clay Diorama

What it means

A soft, glossy clay miniature world on a warm cream set, with pastel robots and simple clay people acting out each idea. The scenes hold still while clean type and graphics do the moving.

When to use it

Great for friendly explainers and short educational reels. Because the pictures barely move, it relies on a good script and strong text animation to stay interesting.
