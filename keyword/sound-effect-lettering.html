Sound Effect Lettering

What it means

Big, bursting comic words like POW, BOOM and CRASH, drawn in chunky shapes with outlines and jagged edges. The sound becomes part of the picture.

When to use it

Use for action, comedy and anything loud. Short words work best; longer ones often get misspelled.
