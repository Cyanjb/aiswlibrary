Southern Gothic

What it means

Stories set in the American South, where the beauty is a little rotten. Peeling mansions, Spanish moss, sticky heat, porch lights and strange, haunted families.

When to use it

Use for eerie, humid, slow-burning scenes with faded grandeur. Keep it subtle, the spookiness comes from atmosphere, not monsters.

Try this
"southern gothic, decaying plantation house, spanish moss, dusk heat haze"
