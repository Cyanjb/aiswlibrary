Web and App Design Styles

Spacious Web Design

What it means

Generous white space, even padding and a steady grid make every section feel airy and easy to read.

When to use it

Use for portfolios, wellness, galleries and any site with lots of reading. Needs strong content, or it can feel empty.

Try this
"website for a yoga retreat, spacious web design, wide margins, soft beige"
