Speech Bubble

What it means

The rounded balloon with a little tail that shows who is talking in a comic. Spiky ones shout, cloudy ones think, wobbly ones whisper.

When to use it

Use to make any image feel like a comic panel. Keep the words to a few, since AI lettering can still come out jumbled.

Try this
"cat in a spacesuit, speech bubble saying hello, comic panel"
