Speed Lines

What it means

Sharp streaks bursting out from or racing behind a subject to show speed, impact or shock. Classic manga shorthand for 'something big just happened.'

When to use it

Use for action, surprise and comedy beats. It's a drawn effect, so it fits illustrated styles better than photos.
