Comic Books

Splash Page

What it means

A full comic page given to one huge, dramatic picture, usually a hero arriving or a big battle. No small panels, just one giant moment.

When to use it

Use for epic, poster-like comic art where everything feels big. Works best with one clear subject and a strong pose.

Try this
"splash page, masked heroine landing on a rooftop, city below, dynamic ink"
