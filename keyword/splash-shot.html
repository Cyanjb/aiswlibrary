Splash Shot

What it means

A drink or fruit frozen mid-splash, with droplets and liquid crowns flying in the air. Fresh, bold and full of energy.

When to use it

Use for drinks, fruit and juicy, refreshing ads. Pair with slow motion for video, or a frozen moment for stills.
