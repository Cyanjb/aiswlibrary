Web Motion and Effects

Staggered Reveal

What it means

The words of a headline rise into place one after another, followed by the buttons, so the page seems to arrive in a gentle wave.

When to use it

Use it for a page opening or a new section coming into view. Keep the delays short so the whole reveal finishes in about a second.

Try this
"Animate my hero so each word of the headline slides up into place one after another, then the buttons fade in after them."
