Stained Glass Animation

What it means

A window being made and lit: thick dark lead lines draw the design, then jewel-colored panes light up one by one, and a slow band of light passes behind them like the sun moving.

When to use it

Beautiful for openers, holiday cards, faith or heritage stories and calm logo reveals. It is slow and ornate, so it does not suit quick edits or lots of text.

Try this
"12-second opener, stained glass animation, lead lines drawing a rose window, jewel panes lighting up one by one"
Pairs well with

stained glass · jewel tones · volumetric light
