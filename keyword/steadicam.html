Camera Movements

Steadicam

What it means

Stabilized floating movement that follows subjects smoothly through space, no track required.

When to use it

Use for long following shots through doorways, corridors, and crowds.

Try this
"steadicam following him through the backstage corridors"
Pairs well with

tracking-shot · long-take
