Food and Cooking Effects

Steam Rising

What it means

Soft wisps of steam curling up from hot food or drink. It tells the eye this is fresh and warm.

When to use it

Use on soups, coffee, noodles and anything that should look hot. It shows up best against a dark background with light from the side or behind.

Try this
"cup of black coffee, steam rising, dark background, side light"

ramen bowl · rim light · low-key lighting
