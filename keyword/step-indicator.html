Step Indicator

What it means

A row of numbered circles joined by a line that shows where you are in a process with several steps. Finished steps turn into ticks and the line fills up as you move along.

When to use it

Use it for sign-up flows, checkouts or set-up wizards so people know how much is left. With more than about five steps it starts to feel long, so group steps where you can.
