Web Motion and Effects

Sticky Header

What it means

The top bar of a page with the logo and menu stays pinned in place while everything else scrolls underneath. Once you start scrolling it shrinks a little and gains a soft shadow.

When to use it

Use it on long pages so the menu and main button are always one click away. On small phone screens keep it slim so it does not eat the view.
