Techniques

Stippling

What it means

Pictures built entirely from tiny dots. Dots packed close together make shadows; spread-out dots make light.

When to use it

Use for old-book illustrations, botanical studies and ink portraits. It reads best in black and white with one clear subject.

Try this
"stippling ink drawing of a barn owl, white paper"
