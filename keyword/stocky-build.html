Stocky Build

What it means

A short to medium height body that is solid, thick-set and sturdy, with a strong chest and wide stance. Grounded and powerful rather than tall.

When to use it

Use for blacksmiths, chefs, farmers, wrestlers or any character who should feel solid and steady. Works for all genders.
