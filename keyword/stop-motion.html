Stop Motion

What it means

Real little objects moved a tiny bit at a time and photographed frame by frame. Everything has a handmade, slightly jittery charm.

When to use it

Use for cozy, crafty scenes with puppets, felt or toys. In video, expect a choppy rhythm; that's part of the magic.
