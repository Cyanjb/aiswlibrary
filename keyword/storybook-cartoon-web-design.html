Web and App Design Styles

Storybook Cartoon Web Design

What it means

A cheerful, cartoon-like site inspired by children's picture books: warm cream backgrounds, big bold headings, bright color blocks, thick black outlines and very rounded shapes.

When to use it

Use for kids' products, picture books, family brands and playful apps. Too childlike for serious or premium brands.

"website for a children's bookshop, storybook cartoon web design, thick black outlines, bright blocks"
