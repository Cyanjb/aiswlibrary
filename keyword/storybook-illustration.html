What it means

The gentle, detailed pictures you find in a children's picture book. Soft colors, friendly shapes, and a scene that feels like it's mid-story.

When to use it

Use for warm, wholesome images that feel like a bedtime tale. Add a medium like watercolor or gouache to steer the look.
