Storytelling Web Design

What it means

A site that tells a story as you scroll, mixing big visuals, short punchy writing, Abril Fatface headlines and gentle animations that carry you from one moment to the next.

When to use it

Use for charities, brand stories, product launches and personal journeys. It takes longer to read, so give visitors a quick way to skip ahead.
