Subsurface Scattering

What it means

The soft glow you see when light sinks into something and lights it from inside, like a backlit ear, a grape or a wax candle.

When to use it

Use to make skin, fruit, jade or wax feel alive instead of plastic. It is subtle, so pair it with backlight to really see it.

"jade dragon figurine, subsurface scattering, backlit"
