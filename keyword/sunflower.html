Sunflower

What it means

Tall stems carrying big golden faces with dark centers, all turned toward the light. Happy, warm and summery.

When to use it

Use for cheerful countryside scenes, portraits in a field and warm yellow palettes. Late afternoon sun makes the petals glow.
