Sunglasses Portal

What it means

The next scene plays reflected in a pair of sunglasses, and putting them on takes us there.

When to use it

Use it for fashion, music or a change of mood. Keep the reflected scene simple so it still reads in the small lenses.

Try this
"a beach at sunset plays reflected in a pair of sunglasses; she puts them on and the camera pulls back to show her now on the beach"
