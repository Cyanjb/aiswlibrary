What it means

A joyful, bouncy partner dance with spins, kicks and lifts, straight out of old dance halls. Smiles and flying skirts.

When to use it

Use for happy, vintage party scenes and big energy. Lifts and flips can tangle limbs, so keep it to spins and kicks.
