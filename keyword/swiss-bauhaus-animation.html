Swiss Bauhaus Animation

What it means

A modernist poster that builds itself: circles, squares and bars in primary colors slide along a strict grid and lock into place, with clean left-aligned type snapping to the same lines. Moves are straight and snappy, never diagonal.

When to use it

Ideal for design-minded brand intros, title cards and clean explainers. It is strict and cool, so it can feel cold for warm or emotional stories.
