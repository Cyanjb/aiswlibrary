Sword and Sorcery

What it means

Rough, muscle-and-magic adventure stories: lone warriors, ancient ruins, wicked sorcerers and treasure guarded by something terrible.

When to use it

Use for bold, painted-paperback fantasy with plenty of action and danger. It is more gritty and personal than grand, kingdom-saving epic fantasy.

Try this
"sword and sorcery warrior in a jungle ruin, glowing idol, painted paperback cover"
