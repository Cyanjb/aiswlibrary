Syrup Drizzle

What it means

A thin, glossy ribbon of syrup or honey flowing down onto food. It catches the light like liquid amber.

When to use it

Use on pancakes, waffles and desserts for a sticky, sweet moment. Backlight makes the drizzle glow.
