Camera Movements

Tabletop Product Shot

What it means

A slow push and drift across a styled table that settles on the product.

When to use it

Use it for calm, tidy product footage of about 10 seconds. Name the props, or the table may fill up with clutter.

Try this
"slow push and drift across a linen tabletop with lemons and glasses, settling on a glass perfume bottle"
Pairs well with
