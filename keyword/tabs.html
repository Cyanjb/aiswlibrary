UI Components

Tabs

What it means

A row of labels across the top of a section, like folder tabs. Clicking one slides a coloured underline over to it and swaps the content below.

When to use it

Use it to split related content into a few groups on one page, such as photos, videos and saved items. More than five or six tabs gets crowded.
