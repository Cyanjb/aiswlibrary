Tailored Suit

What it means

A jacket and trousers cut to fit the body just right, with crisp shoulders, clean lines and no extra fabric. Smart, sharp and pulled together.

When to use it

Use for professional portraits, weddings and confident characters of any gender. Add a fabric, like wool or linen, and a color for more control.
