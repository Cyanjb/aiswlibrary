Body Types

Tall and Lanky

What it means

A long, thin body with long arms and legs, often a little gangly. They tower over the crowd and have to duck through doorways.

When to use it

Use for characters with height as a natural part of who they are, from teen athletes to quiet bookworms. Add a scale clue, like a doorway or other people, so the height shows.

Try this
"tall and lanky young man ducking under a low cafe doorway, warm afternoon"
