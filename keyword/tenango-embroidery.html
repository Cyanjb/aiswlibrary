Tenango Embroidery

What it means

Hand-stitched designs from Otomi (Hñähñu) communities around Tenango de Doria in Hidalgo, Mexico. Playful animals, birds and plants in bright thread on white cotton, sometimes all in one color.

When to use it

Use for joyful textile art, pillows, clothing and pattern backgrounds. Name the tradition instead of a vague 'Mexican pattern' to get the real look.
