Terrarium

What it means

A tiny garden sealed inside glass: moss, ferns, pebbles and sometimes a little figurine. A whole world you can hold in your hands.

When to use it

Use for dreamy miniature worlds and cozy shelf scenes. Glass reflections can get busy, so a dark or plain background helps.
