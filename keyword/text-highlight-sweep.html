Web Motion and Effects

Text Highlight Sweep

What it means

A marker-pen colour sweeps across behind the words of a sentence, one line after another, as if someone is highlighting it while you watch. It pulls the eye to the key message.

When to use it

Use it on one important line, such as a promise or a headline, to make it stand out. Used on too much text it stops meaning anything.
