Web Motion and Effects

Text Scramble

What it means

The letters of a headline flicker through random characters, like a code being cracked, before settling into the real words from left to right.

When to use it

Use it for techy, mysterious or playful headlines. Use it on one short line only; on long text it becomes hard to read.

Try this
"Make my headline do a text scramble: each letter flickers through random characters and settles into the real word, left to right."
