Textbook Illustration

What it means

Clear, carefully labeled drawings like the ones in school science and history books: cutaway views, neat arrows and calm, flat colors.

When to use it

Use for explaining how things work, from volcanoes to the water cycle. Labels often come out as nonsense words, so plan to add real text later.
