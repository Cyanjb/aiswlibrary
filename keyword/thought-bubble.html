Thought Bubble

What it means

A puffy cloud above a character's head, joined by little circles, showing what they are secretly thinking or dreaming.

When to use it

Use for daydreams, inner jokes and wishes. Describe what is inside the bubble, or AI may leave it empty.

Try this
"little girl at a window, thought bubble showing a flying whale"
