Camera Movements

Three-Person Dialogue Coverage

What it means

One take that covers three people talking in a room, moving between each face, over-the-shoulder views and a wide.

When to use it

Use it for conversation scenes where everyone needs screen time. It runs about 30 seconds; say who sits where, or faces can blend together.

Try this
"three friends talk at a kitchen table; slow push between them, over-the-shoulder on each, ending on the wide"
Pairs well with

over-the-shoulder · long take · medium shot
