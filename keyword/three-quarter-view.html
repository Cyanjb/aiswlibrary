Three-Quarter View

What it means

The face or body turned halfway between front and side, so you see both eyes and the curve of the cheek. The classic, most flattering portrait angle.

When to use it

Use for natural portraits and character art that feels more alive than straight on. For a full side view, use profile shot instead.
