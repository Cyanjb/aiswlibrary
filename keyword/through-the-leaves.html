Photo Angles

Through the Leaves

What it means

The photo is taken from behind blurry leaves or flowers close to the lens, so the subject peeks out from soft green shapes.

When to use it

Use for candid, hidden-observer moments and lush nature portraits. Ask for the leaves out of focus so they frame instead of block.

Try this
"woman reading on a bench, shot through the leaves, soft green blur"

bokeh · frame within a frame · candid
