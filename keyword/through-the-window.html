Through the Window

What it means

Looking at the subject through glass, with reflections, raindrops or a window frame between you. It feels private, quiet and a little cinematic.

When to use it

Use for cozy, lonely or watching-from-outside moods. Add rain or steam on the glass for extra feeling.

Try this
"woman reading in a cafe, seen through the window, rain on glass, night"
