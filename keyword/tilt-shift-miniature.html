Tilt Shift Miniature

What it means

A real street or beach seen from above that suddenly looks like a tiny toy model, with a thin strip of sharp focus, dreamy blur at the top and bottom, bright toy colors and little cars and people zipping about in quick bursts.

When to use it

Charming for travel reels, city promos and event recaps. It works best on high-angle shots, and it looks odd on close-ups or eye-level footage.

Try this
"15-second travel reel, tilt shift miniature, busy market from above looking like a toy model, people zipping around"

miniature · diorama · high angle
