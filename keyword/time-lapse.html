Camera Movements

Time-Lapse

What it means

Hours squeezed into seconds: the camera holds still while clouds race, shadows sweep and crowds flow like water.

When to use it

Use to show time passing, a city waking up, or a sky changing. Keep the camera locked so only the world moves.

Try this
"time-lapse of clouds racing over the city skyline from day into night, the camera holds still"
Pairs well with

static hold · aerial shot · tilt shift miniature
