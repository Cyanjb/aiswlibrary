Tintype

What it means

An old portrait printed straight onto a thin metal plate, popular in the 1860s. Dark silvery tones, soft edges and serious faces staring right at you.

When to use it

Use for haunting old-time portraits and Western or Victorian characters. Expect a dark, moody result, not a cheerful one.

"tintype portrait of a cowboy, 1860s, scratched plate edges"
