Tiptoeing

What it means

Creeping along on the very tips of the toes, shoulders hunched, trying not to make a sound. Instantly sneaky and funny.

When to use it

Use for playful mischief, cartoon sneaking and bedtime scenes. Add a sleeping pet or parent to show what they are sneaking past.
