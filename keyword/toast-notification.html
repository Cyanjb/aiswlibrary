Toast Notification

What it means

A small message card that slides in near the corner of the screen to say something happened, then slides away by itself. It pops up like toast from a toaster.

When to use it

Use it to confirm quick actions like Saved or Copied without stopping the person. Do not use it for errors people must fix, because it disappears.
