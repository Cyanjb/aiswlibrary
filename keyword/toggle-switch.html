Toggle Switch

What it means

An on and off switch shaped like a small pill. Clicking it slides the round knob across and the track changes colour to show it is on.

When to use it

Use it for settings that take effect straight away, like dark mode. For choices that only apply after pressing Save, a tick box is clearer.
