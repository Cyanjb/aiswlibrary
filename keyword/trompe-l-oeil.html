Illusions

Trompe l'Oeil

What it means

French for 'fools the eye'. A flat painting so realistic it looks like real windows, doors or objects popping out of the wall.

When to use it

Use for murals, painted ceilings and walls that seem to open up. It shines most when you show the flat wall around the painting.

"trompe l'oeil mural of an open window to the sea on a brick wall"
