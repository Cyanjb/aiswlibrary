Architectural Styles

Tudor Revival

What it means

Storybook houses with dark wooden beams crossing white walls, steep pointed roofs, tall chimneys and small diamond-paned windows.

When to use it

Use for fairytale villages, English cottages and cozy old manor houses. Can look like a theme park if everything is too clean, so add age.
