Turquoise

What it means

A sky-blue to green stone, often with dark web-like veins running through it. Loved for centuries in jewelry from Persia, Tibet and the American Southwest.

When to use it

Use for bold silver jewelry, desert styling and earthy color accents. Name the culture or style you want, since turquoise traditions are rich and specific.
