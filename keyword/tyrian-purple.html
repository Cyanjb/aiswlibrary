Tyrian Purple

What it means

An ancient reddish purple dye made from sea snails. It was so costly to make that it became the color of emperors and kings.

When to use it

Use for royal robes, rich fabrics and ancient feeling scenes. The AI may just give a regular purple, so add 'deep reddish purple'.

Try this
"queen in tyrian purple robes, torchlit marble hall"

opulent · velvet · jewel tones
