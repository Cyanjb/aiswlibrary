Motion Graphics Styles

Ukiyo-e

What it means

Old Japanese woodblock prints come to life: each shot is one finished print on soft paper, flat color blocks slightly off their black lines, a fading sky and a red seal. The camera slides from print to print like unrolling a scroll, with only small things moving.

When to use it

Beautiful for travel, food, culture and calm storytelling. Most of the frame stays still on purpose, so do not expect big action.

Try this
"20-second travel story, ukiyo-e animation, woodblock print of a wave and mountain, rain falling, camera sliding to the next print"
