Advanced Color Spectrum

Ultramarine

What it means

A deep, glowing blue first made by grinding the stone lapis lazuli. In the Renaissance it could cost more than gold.

When to use it

Use for heavenly skies, sacred art and rich blue robes. It leans slightly purple, so ask for cobalt blue if you want a cleaner blue.
