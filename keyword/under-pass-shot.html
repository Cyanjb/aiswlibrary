Camera Movements

Under-Pass Shot

What it means

The camera flies low, right under or between the subject, and out the other side.

When to use it

Use it for impossible shots no real camera crew could get. Give the subject clear space underneath, like a raised truck or a jumping horse.

Try this
"the camera flies low, passes right under a parked truck and out the other side, then looks back"
Pairs well with

fpv drone · ground level · push-through
