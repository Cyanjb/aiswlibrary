Camera Movements

Undercut Snap

What it means

Starts at ankle height, snaps up to the face, glides around, then snaps back down.

When to use it

Use it to go from a small detail to the whole person with some punch. It works best on a subject standing fairly still.

Try this
"start at ankle height on a boxer's shoes, snap up to his face, glide round, snap back down"
Pairs well with

tilt up · ground level · whip pan

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
