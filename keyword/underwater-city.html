Underwater City

What it means

A whole town under the sea, with glass domes, glowing windows and fish drifting past the streets.

When to use it

Use for dreamy fantasy and sci-fi worlds. Add light words like light rays or bioluminescence, or it can look murky.

Try this
"underwater city of glass domes, light rays, schools of fish"
Pairs well with

bioluminescence · volumetric light · seascape
