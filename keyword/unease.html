Emotions and Moods

Unease

What it means

That quiet feeling that something isn't right. Too-empty rooms, a door left open, a figure standing a little too still at the end of the hall.

When to use it

Use for gentle creepiness without monsters or gore. Small strange details work better than one big scary thing.

Try this
"unease, empty motel corridor at night, one door ajar, flickering light"
