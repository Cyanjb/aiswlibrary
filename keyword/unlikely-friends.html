Storytelling

Unlikely Friends

What it means

Two characters who shouldn't get along, sharing a quiet moment together. A tiny mouse and a huge bear, a knight and a dragon having tea.

When to use it

Use for heartwarming, funny or gentle storytelling images. The bigger the difference between them, the sweeter it reads.

Try this
"unlikely friends, wolf and lamb sharing an umbrella in the rain"
