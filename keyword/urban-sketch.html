Motion Graphics Styles

Urban Sketch

What it means

A sketchbook page drawn on location: thin brown pen lines that wobble and never quite close, then see-through watercolor drops in on top, spilling past the lines and fading into cream paper at the edges.

When to use it

Great for travel reels, city guides, cafes and architecture stories. It is loose and arty, so it is not the right pick for precise technical diagrams.
