Vantablack

What it means

One of the darkest materials ever made, a black so deep it swallows almost all light. Objects coated in it look like flat holes cut out of the world.

When to use it

Use for eerie, mind-bending objects and dramatic contrast. AI can only fake it, so expect a very deep black rather than true lightlessness.
