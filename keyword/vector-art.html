Vector Art

What it means

Clean, smooth shapes with sharp edges and flat or simple gradient fills, like a modern app illustration or a logo. No brush texture at all.

When to use it

Use for icons, stickers, posters and tidy graphics. It can look generic, so give it a strong palette or an unusual subject.
