Verdigris

What it means

The chalky blue-green crust that grows on old copper, brass and bronze, like on old statues and church roofs.

When to use it

Use for aged metal, fantasy ruins and steampunk details. It is a texture as much as a color, so it shines on metal surfaces.

"verdigris copper dome above a foggy old city"
