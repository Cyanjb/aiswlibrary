TV and Movie Culture

VHS Horror

What it means

Scary movie footage that looks like it was played on a worn-out home video tape. Fuzzy colors, wobbly lines, soft blur and a creepy, homemade feeling.

When to use it

Use when you want horror that feels found, cheap and a little too real. It hides detail on purpose, so it is not the choice for crisp images.

Try this
"vhs horror still, empty hallway at night, tape glitches, washed colors"
