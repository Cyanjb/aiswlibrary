Vibrant Web Design

What it means

A lively, colorful site with bold, playful Fascinate headings, warm accent colors and lots of visual energy.

When to use it

Use for festivals, food, kids' brands and summer campaigns. Needs calm spaces between sections or it can feel overwhelming.
