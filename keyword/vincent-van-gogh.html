Painters

Vincent van Gogh

What it means

(1853-1890) Dutch painter of swirling skies, thick bold brushstrokes and glowing yellows and blues. Everything feels alive and moving.

When to use it

Use for expressive night skies, sunflowers and countryside scenes full of energy. It is very recognizable, so it can take over a prompt.

Try this
