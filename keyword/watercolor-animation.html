Watercolor Animation

What it means

A nature journal that paints itself: see-through brush strokes with dry-brush streaks on warm cotton paper, big shapes first and small details last, with a quiet handwritten note beside each subject. Finished plants sway gently.

When to use it

Lovely for nature, travel, wellness and gentle educational videos. Painting in takes time, so it does not suit fast cuts.
