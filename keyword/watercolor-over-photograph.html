Watercolor Over Photograph

What it means

A real photo with loose watercolor washes painted on top, so parts of the picture melt into soft, colorful paint.

When to use it

Use to make a photo feel like a memory or a travel journal page. Faces can get blurred, so say where the paint should go.

Try this
"street photo of Venice, watercolor over photograph, paint bleeding at the edges"
Pairs well with

watercolor · film photography · pastel palette
