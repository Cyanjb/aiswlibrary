Style Suffixes

-wave

What it means

Borrowed from music names like vaporwave and synthwave, -wave suggests a retro, nostalgic mood with a strong color palette. Often glowy, dreamy and a little surreal.

When to use it

Use when you want an image to feel like a remembered era or a soundtrack. Results lean pink, purple and neon, so name other colors if you want them.

Try this
"synthwave desert highway, glowing sunset, grid horizon"
