Wet-on-Wet

What it means

Wet paint dropped onto paper that is already wet, so colors bloom, bleed and feather into each other. Soft, unpredictable and lovely.

When to use it

Use for skies, misty backgrounds, flowers and dreamy moods. You give up control, so don't expect sharp lines.

Try this
"wet-on-wet watercolor of a stormy sea sky"
