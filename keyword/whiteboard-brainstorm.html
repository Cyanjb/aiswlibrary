Whiteboard Brainstorm

What it means

A team gathered around a whiteboard covered in sticky notes, arrows and quick sketches, pointing and thinking out loud.

When to use it

Use for teamwork, planning and creative problem-solving images. Writing on the board will usually be gibberish, so keep it in soft focus or crop it out.
