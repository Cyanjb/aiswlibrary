Wild Poppies

What it means

Bright red, paper-thin petals with dark centers, nodding in tall grass. Delicate, cheerful and a little bit wild.

When to use it

Use for summer meadows, nostalgic countryside and pops of red. They look lovely swaying in the wind on video.

Try this
"meadow of wild poppies swaying in the wind, golden hour"
