Wireframe Render

What it means

The 3D object is drawn as a see-through mesh of thin glowing lines, like the skeleton of a computer model.

When to use it

Use for tech, sci-fi and blueprint moods. It reads best on a dark background, and busy scenes can turn into a tangle of lines.

Try this
"sports car wireframe render, cyan lines on black"
