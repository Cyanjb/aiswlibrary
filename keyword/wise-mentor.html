Wise Mentor

What it means

The calm, older guide who teaches the hero, often with a walking staff, a long beard or a cup of tea. Patient, knowing and a little mysterious.

When to use it

Use for fantasy, training and coming-of-age scenes. Try unexpected ages, settings or styles so it doesn't feel stale.
