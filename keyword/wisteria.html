Wisteria

What it means

Long, hanging clusters of purple or white flowers dripping from vines over arches and walls. Like a flower waterfall.

When to use it

Use for fairytale gardens, romantic walkways and dreamy spring scenes. Light coming through the blooms adds magic.

"wisteria tunnel in full bloom, soft light through purple flowers"
