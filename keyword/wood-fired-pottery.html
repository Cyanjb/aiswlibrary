Pottery and Ceramics

Wood-Fired Pottery

What it means

Pots baked for days in a wood-burning kiln, so flying ash melts into drippy glaze and flame marks paint warm browns, oranges and greens.

When to use it

Use for rustic, natural pottery that looks touched by fire. Every surface is uneven, which is the charm.
