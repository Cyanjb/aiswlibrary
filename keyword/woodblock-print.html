Printing and Transfer Techniques

Woodblock Print

What it means

Pictures printed from carved wooden blocks, one block per color, with flat areas of color and wood grain sometimes showing through.

When to use it

Use for Japanese-style landscapes, waves and gentle folk scenes. Pairs well with soft natural colors.

Try this
"woodblock print of a mountain village in snow, indigo and red"
