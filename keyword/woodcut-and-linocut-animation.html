Motion Graphics Styles

Woodcut and Linocut Animation

What it means

A carved relief print coming to life: bold shapes in one or two inks stamp down onto textured paper with a heavy press, and carving lines cut themselves in. Each layer lands with a thump, slightly speckled and a little off.

When to use it

Strong for folk tales, craft brands, music videos and anything that wants a handmade, earthy weight. It is chunky by design, so fine detail and soft gradients get lost.
