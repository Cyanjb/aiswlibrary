Worm's-Eye View

What it means

The view from the very ground looking straight up, as if you were a worm. Trees, buildings and people tower over you.

When to use it

Use to make anything look huge, heroic or dizzying. It is more extreme than a plain low angle.

Try this
"worm's-eye view of skyscrapers and a lone tree, blue sky"

low angle · wide angle lens · cityscape
