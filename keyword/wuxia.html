Wuxia

What it means

Chinese martial arts adventure where swordsmen and swordswomen glide across rooftops, bamboo tops and lakes. Flowing robes, misty mountains and graceful, floating fights.

When to use it

Use for elegant action that feels more like dance than brawling. Great with slow motion and fog for that drifting, weightless look.
