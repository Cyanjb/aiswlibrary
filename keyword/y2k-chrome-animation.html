Y2K Chrome Animation

What it means

A shiny millennium dream: liquid chrome blobs melt into hearts and stars, mirror type glints as a highlight sweeps across it, and bubbly windows pop open over a candy pink and baby blue sky.

When to use it

Great for fashion, beauty and music reels with a nostalgic, glossy feel. The shine is the whole point, so it can look cheap if the chrome is flat or dull.

Try this
"10-second beauty reel, y2k chrome animation, liquid chrome hearts melting, glinting mirror type on a candy pink sky"
