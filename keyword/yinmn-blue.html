Advanced Color Spectrum

YInMn Blue

What it means

A vivid, electric blue pigment discovered by accident in a university lab in 2009. Named after the elements in it: yttrium, indium and manganese.

When to use it

Use when you want a blue that feels brighter and more modern than royal blue. The AI may not know it well, so add 'vivid electric blue' too.

Try this
"ceramic vase glazed in YInMn blue, vivid electric blue, white studio"
