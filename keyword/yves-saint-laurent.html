Yves Saint Laurent

What it means

French designer Yves Saint Laurent (1936-2008) put women in tuxedos with his famous Le Smoking suit, and loved safari jackets, sheer blouses and bold blocks of color inspired by art.

When to use it

Use for confident, sleek 1960s and 1970s Parisian style. Great for tuxedo looks and dramatic black and white fashion portraits.
