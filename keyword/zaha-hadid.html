Architects

Zaha Hadid

What it means

Iraqi-British architect (1950-2016) known for buildings that swoop and flow like liquid, with no straight corners. Smooth white curves that look like they are in motion.

When to use it

Use for sleek, futuristic buildings and spaces that feel fast even standing still. Too much of it can look like a generic sci-fi set, so add a real place or material.

Try this
"opera house of flowing white curves at dusk, Zaha Hadid style"
