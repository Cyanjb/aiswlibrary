Camera Movements

Zoom

What it means

The camera stays still and only the lens zooms, so the background flattens as it closes in.

When to use it

Use for a watchful, documentary feel, or a 1970s film look.

Try this
"slow zoom in from the wide street to her face in the crowd"
Pairs well with

snap-zoom · dolly-zoom

Works in

seedance · kling · veo · runway
